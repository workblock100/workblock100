# GUIDE_ART22: the Episode 22 art kit (`window.A22`, `ep22_art.js`)

Holiday wear (Santa hats for the whole cast, the Administrator as Santa), the gifts and their paper, the Chux pad, the tree and its lights, then the props of the wrap, the search, the swap and the carol. Contract: `/tmp/shift_tools/briefs/A22_API.md` (names and shapes fixed; the options below are additions). Everything is a pure function of its arguments and `t` (no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no state); randomness only from `FX18.rnd`; `ctx` saved and restored; text only through the UI kit, drawn live. Every `(ctx, o)` prop draws in the CURRENT transform and returns its box `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` in those units (the tree returns `{ box, lights }`). Bench: `node a22_bench.js <view>` renders `build/ep22_art/out/<view>.png` (views: `hats`, `hatturn`, `santa`, `santaturn`, `props1`, `tree`, `det`, `cost`).

Check at DRAW time, never at load: `window.A22 && A22.santa` (the load order is not yours).

## Batch 1: the hats, Santa, the gifts and the paper, the Chux, the tree

### `A22.santaHat(o, g)` -> `o`
Puts a red Santa hat (`fp22_santahat`: a velvet cone that flops to one side and sways with `t`, a fluffy white fur band, a white pom at the tip) on ANY `drawChar` options object (A7, A11, A15, A20, A21, the residents). Options on `o` or in `g` (`g` wins): `hatFlop` 0..1 (0 upright, 1 the tip hangs down beside the head; default .7, Onion .2), `hatSide` +1 / -1 (the tip falls to screen right / left; default by `o.who`), `hatColor` (default `'#d42a2a'`; `A22.ELF` = `'#2f9a46'` for an elf hat), `bandColor`, `pomColor`, `hatTall` (+px), `hatLift` (+px up), `hatMaxW`, `sway` (default 1; 0 = still), `wind` -1..1, `hatTilt` (radians). Call it AFTER the constructor and BEFORE you merge `x, y, s, expr`; call it every frame (the tip sways with `o.t`).
What it changes: `o.acc` gets `fp22_santahat` (a new array); Strawberry's nurse cap is off (`cap: false`); whatever sits where a hat goes is dropped (every engine `head`-class accessory but `airpods`, the visor, Onion's visor badge, Tomato's headset, hard hats, party hats, A20 hats and caps, the bandage); `o.topExtra` grows so the audit's head box includes the cone and the pom; `o.h22` holds the resolved options. The band sits 21 px above the brow line (plus `browUp`): it never touches the face box. Onion's sprout pokes out of the pom; the Binder wears a small one on its top edge. Turns: a head-class accessory on `V2C.YAW_ACC` (front, three-quarter, profile, back: in profile the tip swings to the back of the head, never over the face).
```js
const o = A22.santaHat(A15.tomato({ t: lt }), { hatFlop: .8 }); drawChar(Object.assign(o, { x: 540, y: 1316, s: .9, expr: 'tom_fruit' }));
drawChar(Object.assign(A22.santaHat(A7.pear({ t: lt, noLegs: true })), { x: 300, y: 1200, s: .6 }));   // a resident in a wheelchair
```

### `A22.santa(o)` -> `drawChar` options (`who: 'melon'`, kind `melon`)
The Administrator (Melon) as SANTA: the hat (flopped to screen right), a white curly beard and moustache (drawn UNDER the face: his eyes, rosy cheeks and moving mouth read on top; the beard never reaches the eyes and its sideburns stay outside the face box), a red coat with a white fur hem and front strip, a black belt with a gold buckle round the round body (the melon IS the belly), red sleeves and trousers with white fur cuffs, black boots. Options: `pose` `'stand'` (default) | `'seated'` | `'hoyer'`; `sack` `false` (default) | `'hand'` (the sack `fp22_sack` hangs from the right hand) | `'shoulder'` (slung over the right shoulder, behind the body); `expr` any of Melon's plus `santa_jolly` (the default: a jolly squint) and `santa_stuck` (eyebrows up, a worried little smile: sunk in the chair); `hat` (an object of `santaHat` options, or `false`); `beard` (true); `yaw`, `headYaw` (the coat strip and buckle travel round the body, the beard is sphere-mapped with the face); everything `drawChar` takes.
- `'seated'`: in a LOW armchair, knees up. `x, y` = the floor under his boots; the body sits 70 local units above it (`seat`), the knees come up in front of his belly, the boots rest on the floor. Draw the chair behind him and its arms after him: `A22.lowChair(ctx, { x, y, s, part: 'back' })`, Santa, then `A22.lowChair(ctx, { x, y, s, part: 'front' })`.
- `'hoyer'`: curled up in the lift's sling: `x, y` = the CENTRE of his body (`center: true`), knees tucked up, boots dangling, both arms up and out holding the straps (pass `grip: { l, r }` in his local units to put the hands on the straps; `A22.santaGrip(o, [sx, sy])` converts a screen point). The scene draws the SOFT2D sling over his lower half.
```js
drawChar(Object.assign(A22.santa({ t: lt, sack: 'hand' }), { x: 540, y: 1300, s: 1, look: [.3, 0] }));           // "Ho ho ho!"  (who 'melon': the mouth moves)
A22.lowChair(ctx, { x: 540, y: 1320, s: 1, part: 'back' }); drawChar(Object.assign(A22.santa({ t: lt, pose: 'seated', expr: 'santa_stuck' }), { x: 540, y: 1320, s: 1 })); A22.lowChair(ctx, { x: 540, y: 1320, s: 1, part: 'front' });
```
Traps: the red sleeves, trousers and fur cuffs are a colour swap done by ONE wrapper of the global `drawChar` (guarded, pass-through for anyone else, like A20's mittens): a scene drawing through a captured copy of `drawChar` gets Santa with dark arms. `A22.santa` drops Melon's suit and name tag. Speaking size: `s` 1 is 256 px wide and about 520 px tall with the hat.

### `A22.gift(ctx, o)` -> box
A wrapped box, front view with the top face showing. `o = { x, y (the BOTTOM centre, on the table), s, rot, t, w (180), h (140), d (depth of the top face, .3 x w), paper 'stripes'|'dots'|'trees'|'candy'|'plain', color, color2, bow (true), ribbon (true), ribbonColor, bowColor, tag (string: a hanging tag, UI-kit text, dressing unless `tagHero`), tagSide, open 0..1 (the lid lifts and tips back), seed }`. `A22.GIFT_COLORS` holds the episode's papers. `A22.giftInfo(o)` returns `{ box, bow: [x, y], lid, tag }` without drawing (for a match cut on the bow).
```js
A22.gift(ctx, { x: 540, y: 1120, s: 1.6, w: 220, h: 170, paper: 'stripes', color: '#c62828', color2: '#f4e9d8', ribbonColor: '#e8b93a', tag: 'TO: THE 3-11', t: lt });
```

### `A22.paperPrint(kind, color, color2)` -> `(ctx, w, h) => {}`
The print of a wrapping paper drawn in rest space `0..w x 0..h` on the context it is GIVEN (for `SOFT2D.drawCloth`'s `fill`), the same print `A22.gift` uses, so a simulated paper and a drawn gift look the same. Kinds as `gift`; the callback caches one sprite per (kind, colours, size) and blits it. `A22.paperBack` = the plain white reverse (`back:` in `drawCloth`).

### `A22.chux(ctx, o)` -> box and `A22.chuxPrint()` -> `(ctx, w, h) => {}`
The blue disposable underpad, flat: a quilted blue field (a diamond quilt of stitched channels), a white border, a faint plastic sheen. `o = { x, y (centre), s, rot, t, w (300), h (220), fold 0..1 (the far edge folds over toward us), crumple 0..1 }`. `chuxPrint()` is its print for `SOFT2D.drawCloth` (the back of a Chux is white plastic: `A22.chuxBack`).

### `A22.tree(ctx, o)` -> `{ box, lights: [{ x, y, color, phase, r }], star: [x, y] }` and `A22.twinkle(t, phase)` -> 0..1
A decorated Christmas tree: layered boughs, a tinsel garland, baubles, a star, a red tree skirt and a pot. `o = { x, y (the floor under the trunk), s, h (520 local px), t, lights (true), baubles (true), tinsel (true), star (true), skirt (true), gifts (0: wrapped boxes under it), glow (.55: a small halo per bulb drawn here; pass 0 if the scene lights them through L20), lit 0..1 (the lights' level), seed }`. `lights: true` draws the string lights and RETURNS them in the units of the coordinates you passed (the frame `x, y` are in: world units, the same as `L20.pass` lights; `r` = the bulb radius in those units) for L20 point lights and bloom; each bulb's level is `A22.twinkle(t, phase)`: a slow sine (0.3 to 0.8 Hz, the frequency picked from the phase), never in sync, never below .3. Use the same value for its L20 light.
```js
const T22 = A22.tree(ctx, { x: 860, y: 1300, s: 1, t: lt, glow: 0 });   // ... the rest of the room, the cast ... then, last in draw():
L20.pass(ctx, { ambient: .55, t: lt, lights: T22.lights.filter((b, i) => i % 3 === 0).map(b => ({ kind: 'point', x: b.x, y: b.y, r: 120, color: b.color, power: .5 * A22.twinkle(lt, b.phase) })) });   // a few bulbs as lights (L20 costs by pixel)
```
Cost: a cached bough sprite per (h, seed): about 1 ms per call after the first.

## Batch 2: the wrap, the search, the swap (props)
Every prop is `A22.<name>(ctx, o)` with `o = { x, y, s, rot, t, hero, grp, anchor }` and returns its box `[x0, y0, x1, y1]`. **Anchor**: things that stand on a surface (gloveBox, pudding, candle, mug, dentureCup; and `gift`) take `x, y` = the BOTTOM centre (they rotate about it); everything else takes the CENTRE; `anchor: 'bottom' | 'centre'` overrides. Text on props goes through the UI kit live; it is dressing (`hero: false`) unless you pass `hero: true` AND it is 30 px or more on screen (then the gate holds it to the hero rules); under 7 px it is skipped. Sizes are at `s` 1.
- `gloveBalloon(ctx, o)`: an inflated purple nitrile glove, fingers up, the knot at the bottom (x, y = the palm). `puff` 0..1 (limp .. fat fingers splayed), `face` (a marker smiley on the palm), `color` (`A22.NITRILE`), `side` (-1 mirrors the thumb), `string` (a curly ribbon length from the knot, px), `stringColor`. About 180 x 220. Same look as `SOFT2D.drawBalloon`.
- `glove(ctx, o)`: a flat, uninflated glove (about 110 x 200). `gloveBox(ctx, o)`: NITRILE GLOVES carton (bottom; 170 x 120 with the top face), `peek` 0..1 fingers out of the slot.
- `tapeRoll(ctx, o)`: paper tape, three-quarter view (centre; ring 60 x 76), `pull` 0..1 a strip pulled out to the side (`dir` +1 / -1, `len` 190), torn end.
- `pudding(ctx, o)`: cup with foil lid and SUGAR FREE (bottom; 76 x 62), `open` 0..1 peels the lid, `flavor` 'chocolate' | 'vanilla'. `candle(ctx, o)`: jar candle VANILLA SUGAR COOKIE (bottom; 92 x 108), `lit` 0..1 (a small, slow flame and glow).
- `gripSocks(ctx, o)`: a pair of YELLOW non-skid socks held up by the cuffs (x, y = the cuffs' centre; 220 x 170 below it).
- `mug(ctx, o)`: white mug (bottom; 220 x 210 + handle), `text` (default "WORLD'S OKAYEST NURSE", laid out by `UI.sign` in three lines: WORLD'S / OKAYEST / NURSE), `hero: true` when held up big, `steam` 0..1, `id` (the sign's container id; give each mug its own).
- `dentures(ctx, o)`: false teeth (centre; 100 x 50), `open` 0..1 the gap between the plates (clack them with a fast `open`). `dentureCup(ctx, o)`: translucent blue lidded cup (bottom; 96 x 92), `open` 0..1 (the lid swings up on its hinge), `teeth` (true: the plates show through), `label`.
- `nameSlip(ctx, o)`: a folded slip (centre; 120 wide, 29 folded, 58 open), `unfold` 0..1 (the top half swings up), `name` (written once it is open; italic, `hero` if big).
- `santaHatProp(ctx, o)` / `secretSantaHat(ctx, o)`: a Santa hat held upside down by its brim (x, y = the brim's centre; brim 210 wide, the cone and pom hang 200 below), `slips` (0..14 name slips standing in it; secretSantaHat defaults to 12), `shake` (seconds since a shake began, -1 none: the hat jiggles, the slips bounce), `tilt`, `glow` 0..1 (Binder's magic), `hatColor`. `A22.hatPropInfo(o)` -> `{ box, mouth: [x, y], mouthW, pom }` (where the names fly out).
- Held versions (`hold: { r: 'fp22_mug' }`, options in `o.p22`, e.g. `p22: { puff: 1, face: true }`, `p22.s` scales): `fp22_mug`, `fp22_candle`, `fp22_pudding`, `fp22_socks`, `fp22_dcup`, `fp22_balloon` (on its string above the hand), `fp22_tape`, `fp22_slip`, `fp22_hatprop`, `fp22_glove`; all registered in `PROP_BOX`. A held prop is small (true to size); for a hero close-up draw the set piece yourself at the hand.
```js
A22.mug(ctx, { x: 540, y: 1110, s: 1.2, hero: true, t: lt, id: 'mug_hero' });        // "Read it." (the swap)
A22.secretSantaHat(ctx, { x: 540, y: 760, s: 1.4, slips: 12, shake: lt - 1.2, t: lt });   // the draw hat, brim centred at (540, 760)

## Batch 3: the Hoyer, the gait belt, lights, tinsel, curls, wreath, stockings, the sweater
- `A22.hoyer(ctx, o)` -> `{ box, hooks: [[x, y] x4], tip, pivot, bar: { x, y, rot, a, b }, base }`: the Hoyer lift in side view, the geometry of `ep22_santa_sim.js` `geo.hoyer` (`A22.HOYER` = `{ mastH 640, boom 420, a0 -.24, a1 .36, swivel 40, bar 150, hookIn 118, baseLen 470 }`; pass `geo: {...}` to change any). `o = { x (the mast's foot), y (the floor), s, lift 0..1 (boom angle a0 -> a1) or ang (radians, wins), theta (the bar's sway, radians), spread (1: hook spacing factor), facing (-1 default: the boom reaches LEFT; +1 mirrors), layer 'all' | 'back' (far leg, mast, handle, pendant, actuator, boom) | 'bar' (swivel, spreader bar, hooks) | 'front' (near leg and casters), wheelRot, color }`. Same `x, y, ang, theta` as the sim give the same hooks (pin the SOFT2D sling to them). `A22.hoyerInfo(o)` = the geometry without drawing.
- `A22.gaitBelt(ctx, o)`: `mode 'coil'` (default: a rolled canvas strap with its metal buckle, centre x, y; about 170 x 230) or `mode 'strap'` with `a, b` (points), `sag`, `w` (26): a straight belt, buckle at `a`. `color`, `stripe`.
- `A22.stringLights(ctx, o)` -> `{ box, lights }`: `o = { a: [x, y], b: [x, y], sag (40), n (8), s, t, seed, lit, glow (.5) }`; bulbs hang along a parabola, returned like the tree's (caller units, `phase` for `A22.twinkle`).
- `A22.tinsel(ctx, pts, o)` -> box: a fluffy foil garland along a polyline (a SOFT2D rope's points): `o = { width (16), color ('#e8c04a'; silver '#c8d0dc'), seed, t }`; a few glints fade in and out slowly.
- `A22.ribbonCurl(ctx, o)`: a curling-ribbon helix hanging from `x, y`: `{ color, len (120), turns (3.5), r (12), w (7), s, t }`. `A22.bow(ctx, o)`: a stick-on bow (centre; `r` 60, `color`).
- `A22.wreath(ctx, o)`: outer radius 80 at `s` 1 (centre), fir, berries, a red bow at the bottom (`bowColor`); the ring is cached.
- `A22.stocking(ctx, o)`: hangs from `x, y` (the top of its fur cuff); `name` on the cuff (UI kit; dressing unless `hero`), `color`, `dir` (+1 toe to the right). About 200 x 270.
- `A22.sweater(o, g)` -> `o`: the ugly holiday sweater `fp22_sweater` (wrap; first in `o.acc` so lanyards, badges and hats go over it): from just under the mouth to the hem, pixel-knit bands of trees, a zigzag and snowflakes, a ribbed hem and collar; the pattern travels round with the body yaw. `g = { color '#2f8a4a', color2 '#fbf7ef', color3 '#d62839', lift (px) }`. Works on any kind (made for Tomato: `A22.sweater(A15.tomato({ t }))`).

## Cost, determinism, traps
- Cost per call at `s` 1, measured WITH rasterisation (Chromium software canvas, a flush per call; a plain `A7.straw` is 5.0 ms on the same bench): Santa hat on Strawberry +0.8 ms; `A22.santa` 9.0 ms (yaw .6: 8.2, seated: 9.3); `A22.tree` with lights 5.2 ms (the boughs, tinsel and baubles are one cached sprite per h, seed and resolution; the bulbs are live); `A22.gift` with a tag 2.0 ms; `A22.chux` 0.3 ms; a `paperPrint` blit 0.4 ms. `node a22_bench.js cost`.
- Determinism: `node a22_bench.js det` draws every bench view twice in one page and once in a second page and compares the pixels, with `Math.random` and `Date` trapped: identical, no calls.
- Traps: (1) call `santaHat` / `santa` / `sweater` every frame AFTER the constructor and before merging `x, y, s`; (2) the Santa colours (red sleeves and trousers, fur cuffs, black boots) and Onion's hidden sprout come from ONE wrapper of the global `drawChar`: draw through `drawChar`, not a captured copy; (3) props anchor at the bottom centre when they stand on something (gift, gloveBox, pudding, candle, mug, dentureCup), else at the centre; (4) prop text is dressing unless you pass `hero: true` and it is 30 px or more on screen; give each hero mug its own `id`; (5) `tree`, `stringLights` return bulbs in YOUR units (world units inside the camera transform), ready for `L20.pass`; pass `glow: 0` if L20 lights them; (6) `A22.hoyer` matches the SANTA sim's hooks only with the same `x, y, ang, theta` and `s` 1; (7) `V2.mix` does not parse `rgba()` strings: mix hex colours or arrays.

## The cards (ep22_title.js, scenes `title` and `end`)
On SOFT2D (`ep22_title_sim.js`, `window.__EP22_SIMS.title`): the ribbon is a rope yanked off at `ribbonpull`; the front paper is a cloth printed with `A22.paperPrint` (back `A22.paperBack`) torn by two `w.rip` paths at `paperrip` 1.8 and 2.3, each half gripped along its torn edge, folded over and carried out of frame; ten flat scraps flutter onto the table; built once, sampled with `world.at(min(lt, 4.6))`. Without SOFT2D the scene falls back to a hand-animated stand-in. The title ends with the lid ajar and its red bow centred at (540, 760), about 300 px wide, camera still from 3.4 s. The end card opens on a round red stamp (seen from above) centred at (540, 760), 300 px across, still for 0.3 s; the ink stamp APPROVED PER POLICY lands there at 4.0 s. `TEASER23` at the top of the file holds NEW YEAR'S EVE.
