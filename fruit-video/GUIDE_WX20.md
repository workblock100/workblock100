# GUIDE_WX20: the Episode 20 weather and light kit (`ep20_fx.js`: `window.SN20` snow and cold, `window.L20` light)

Contract: `/tmp/shift_tools/briefs/WX20_API.md` (names and shapes are fixed; this file says what was built, with the additions). Owner: the WX builder. Built on `FX18` (`ep18_fx.js` loads first): `FX18.rnd`, `FX18.noise`, `FX18.sprite`, `FX18.cone`, `FX18.glow`. Guard at DRAW time (`window.SN20 && SN20.snow`, `typeof L20.pass === 'function'`): the load order inside the episode is not yours. EVERY function of the contract has landed. Demo and tests: `wx20_demo.html`, `wx20_test.js` (last section). A scene that uses most of it (a lit desk, a window with snow, frost and a sill bank, the globe's vortex, the pass, `settle` into the title): `ep20_title.js`.

## Rules for every function
- Signature `SN20.name(ctx, o)` / `L20.name(ctx, o)`. WORLD functions draw in the transform you already have (the characters' transform after the scene camera). SCREEN functions (`L20.emergency`) reset the transform and use the whole canvas; call them from `over(lt)` (after the grade); the camera does not move them.
- PURE functions of their arguments and `t` (scene-local seconds, the `lt` the scene gets): no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no state that depends on how many frames were drawn. Randomness only from `FX18.rnd`. The same call twice, or frame t drawn first or last, or in a second worker gives identical pixels (tested, every function, and the title and end cards through the real renderer).
- Each call saves and restores the context state. Soft shapes come from cached sprites. A frame is rendered up to 16 times for motion blur: do not stack many big calls in a moving shot.
- Snow is in-world: behind or beside characters. NEVER over hero text, a face or the caption zone except as a light, small, low-alpha dressing (`fore: false` drops the big near layer; `slice: 'back'` / `'front'` split the field around your characters).
- Readability (LEGIBILITY L4): `tone: 'light'` (default) draws flakes as white puffs shaded blue toward the lower right, with a thin cool rim and a faint halo, so they read on pale walls; `tone: 'dark'` draws plain white flakes with a soft halo for night skies and dark rooms. Nothing white on white: on a pale wall use `tone: 'light'` and keep `alpha` at .8 or more.

## The clock of the snow
- `SN20.SCENE_CLOCK = { forecast: 840, lot: 990, blackout: 1092, dayroom: 1240, night: 1379 }`: the clock minute (after midnight; 2:00 PM = 840) at the START of each scene. A scene's clock at local time `lt` is `SN20.SCENE_CLOCK.<id> + lt / 60` (the night's time-lapse runs 1379 to 1860: pass the lapse clock yourself).
- `SN20.depthAt(clockMinutes)` -> 0..1 accumulated snow depth (a monotone cubic through 0 at 840, .25 at 990, .45 at 1212, .6 at 1360, .9 at 1860; 0 before 840, creeping to 1 after 1860). Use it for every sill, car, roof and frost: `const d = SN20.depthAt(SN20.SCENE_CLOCK.dayroom + lt / 60); SN20.sill(ctx, { ..., depth: 10 + 60 * d })`. The same window is deeper at 8:40 than at 2:00.
- `SN20.wind(t, gusts)` -> -1..1 sideways push: a slow deterministic mix of two incommensurate sines plus the scripted gusts `[[t0, dur, amp], ...]` (a raised-cosine bump of height `amp` from t0 for dur seconds: a door opening, the plow; amp may be negative). `SN20.windInt(t, gusts)` -> px, the integral of the UNCLAMPED wind times 150 px/s (what `snow` uses for drift; keep base plus gusts under 1). `SN20.GUST(t, gusts)` [added] -> the gusts alone. Put a gust at each script cue `doorgust` / `windgust` so the picture and the sound agree.

## Snow and cold
- `SN20.snow(ctx, o)` falling snow in a rectangle. `o = { x0, y0, x1, y1, t, seed, layers (1..4, default 3), density (1 = about 90 flakes per layer in a 1080 x 1920 rect; scales with area; a window pane needs 3 to 6), fall (speed multiplier), wind (px/s sideways, default 0), gusts, sway (0..2, default 1), size (multiplier), alpha (multiplier), tone ('light' | 'dark'), fore (default true: the large soft near layer), slice ('all' | 'back' | 'front'), clip (default true: clip to the rectangle) }`. Layers back to front: far (2.8 to 4.8 px, dim, slow about 60 px/s, twice as many), [mid-far when layers is 4], mid (7 to 12.5 px, about 125 px/s, a fifth of them six-armed crystals), near (14 to 21 px, big, soft, out of focus, about 195 px/s; only with 3 or more layers and `fore`). Every flake sways on two incommensurate frequencies; the whole field leans with `wind` plus the wind curve (near flakes move 1.35 x, far .5 x: parallax). Flakes wrap in an invisible margin outside the rectangle: nothing pops. Flakes are drawn on whole pixels (a blit without filtering costs half). Cost, full frame, 3 layers: 1.4 ms at density 1, 2.5 ms at density 2. `slice: 'back'` draws far and mid (call it before the characters), `'front'` the near layer only (after them, only where it crosses no face and no text). Two fields need different `seed`s. `SN20.snow(ctx, { x0: 0, y0: 0, x1: 1080, y1: 1920, t: lt, seed: 3, density: 2, wind: 120, gusts: [[4.2, 1.2, .8]], tone: 'dark', slice: 'back' })`
- `SN20.frost(ctx, o)` frost on a window pane. `o = { x, y, w, h, amount (0..1), seed, t, tone, fade }` (x, y = top left; `fade` 0..1 scales the misted rim, default 1). Branching ferns (a bending stem, side branches that start once the stem has passed their root, twigs) grow in from the bottom corners first, then along the bottom and the sides, last from the top as `amount` rises; 0 draws nothing; 1 covers about 60 percent of the pane with white lace and a milky rim and leaves the centre clear; two nested diagonal glints travel across the lace with `t`. The pane rectangle is clipped for you. The lace is baked once per pane size, seed, tone and amount step (1/40; the first call at a new step costs about 20 ms once) and the two neighbouring steps are cross-faded, so growing frost is smooth and a call costs one pane-sized blit (1 to 2 ms). `SN20.frost(ctx, { x: 300, y: 500, w: 480, h: 640, amount: Math.min(1, 1.4 * d), seed: 2, t: lt })`
- `SN20.sill(ctx, o)` a snow heap on a ledge. `o = { x0, x1, y (the ledge top), depth (px), seed, tone, t }`. Lumpy profile that tapers at both ends, a soft blue shadow under the lip, a few sparkles (they twinkle if you also pass `t`). Draw it after the ledge. About .1 ms. `SN20.sill(ctx, { x0: 300, x1: 780, y: 1160, depth: 10 + 60 * d, seed: 4 })`
- `SN20.bank(ctx, o)` a drift or mound, steeper on its windward side, lit from the top left, with wind-carved ridges and sparkles. `o = { x, y (base centre), w, h, seed, shade (0..1, how blue the shadow side is, default .6), flip, tone, t }`. About .4 ms.
- `SN20.cap(ctx, o)` snow lying along a flat top (car roof, sign, bench, hat brim). `o = { x0, x1, y (the top surface), depth, seed, round (0..1, default .6), tone, t }`. Draw AFTER the object; the layer sits above `y` and overhangs the edges by a few px. About .15 ms.
- `SN20.beam(ctx, o)` a headlight or flashlight beam in falling snow. `o = { x, y, ang, spread, len, color, alpha, t, seed, flakes (default true), soft, wind, gusts, density }`, built on `FX18.cone`; the flakes inside the beam are brighter and sparkle; they fall like the scene's snow (pass the same `wind` and `gusts` as your `SN20.snow`). Cost is the cone's fill rate (len 800: about 3 ms) plus up to 240 dots: one beam per light.
- `SN20.breath(ctx, o)` a puff of cold breath. `o = { x, y (the mouth), t, seed, period (1.9 s), dir (+1 right, -1 left), size (1 = a 60 px puff), alpha, phase (seconds: shifts the schedule) }`. Six lobes leave over .4 s, drift, rise, swell and fade over about 1.9 s; thicker and softer than steam, with a faint blue-grey edge that shows as a darker rim on a pale wall. About 1 ms.
- `SN20.spray(ctx, o)` snow thrown by a plow blade or a shovel. `o = { x, y, ang (default -pi/4), t, t0, t1, seed, power (default 1), width (rad, default .8), gravity (default 1100) }`. Clumps and fine powder, slowed by the air, plus a cloud at the blade while it throws; emission runs from t0 to t1 and what is in the air finishes its flight. Put it at `A20.plowTruck(...).blade`. About 1 ms.
- `SN20.flurry(ctx, o)` a burst of powder (a snowball hit, a door dump, a slip). `o = { x, y, r (size of the burst, default 80), t, t0, life (default 1), seed, n (default 36) }`: a flash of white and n puffs thrown outward. About 1 ms.
- `SN20.maskPoints(drawFn, w, h, step, seed)` target points sampled from whatever `drawFn(g)` paints into an offscreen w x h canvas (a flat silhouette: the text gate ignores that canvas): a jittered grid of `step` px (default 7) over every inked cell, as `[x, y]` in that canvas's coordinates. Pure and deterministic, but call it ONCE and keep the result, and only when the font is loaded (see `titleMask` in `ep20_title.js`).
- `SN20.settle(ctx, o)` snow settling into a shape (the title). `o = { pts, from: { x, y, r }, t, t0, t1, seed, size (flake diameter, default 7), alpha, tone, max (the most flakes, default 2600) }`. Every flake swirls round `from` in a vortex (the inner ones turn faster) until t0, then flies to its own target point on a curved path, each leaving at its own moment, and rests there from t1 on. About 3 us a flake. Draw the solid UI-kit text after it and fade the flakes out as it comes in.

## Light: `L20.pass(ctx, o)`
A lighting pass over the picture already drawn. Call it as the LAST world-space draw call of the scene's `draw` (camera transform active), or from `over()` with the same transform you used for the world. It darkens everything except what the lights reach, tints the dark, adds each light's colour and an optional bloom, grade and vignette. Positions are in the same units as the characters; the pass converts them through `ctx.getTransform()` (zoom, pan and roll all work: a marker at a light's position stays under it). Returns the number of lights drawn.
- `o = { ambient (0..1: how much of the picture survives outside the lights; .22 dim but readable, 0 pitch black; never below .12 outside the pitch-black beat), tint (the colour of the dark, default '#0b1230'), lights: [...], bloom (0..1, default .5: a glow on the cores of point lights and cone lenses, fed to the engine's bloom), grade ({ color, alpha, mode }: a final wash, mode 'multiply' | 'screen' | 'overlay' | 'soft-light' | 'lighter' | 'source-over'), vignette (0..1, default .25), lift (0..1: a floor under the darkness, default .08: the survival floor is max(ambient, lift)), t (scene-local seconds, drives the flicker), glow (how much of each light's colour is added to the picture, default .32) }`.
- Light kinds, each `{ kind, x, y, color, power (0..1.5, default 1; 0 is skipped), flicker: { seed, amt, rate } }`; `amt` is clamped to .25 and `rate` to 3 Hz, so no setting can become a strobe:
  - `'point'`: `r` (radius px), `soft` (0..1, default .7). A lamp, a candle, a stopwatch, an LED.
  - `'cone'`: `ang` (rad, 0 = +x, pi/2 = down), `spread` (full angle rad), `len`, `soft`. A flashlight, a headlight, a downlight; a small pool lights the hand and the lens.
  - `'rect'`: `x, y` = the CENTRE, `w`, `h`, `soft`; `facing` (rad) optional spill direction, `spill` (px, default 1.4 x the larger side). A window, a TV, a laptop or clip screen. The rectangle itself is fully lit with a soft halo; with `facing` it also throws a soft trapezoid of light in front of itself (the patch a window makes on the floor).
  - `'strip'`: `x, y` to `x2, y2`, `width`, `soft`. A ceiling strip, an exit sign's wash.
- Cost: the darkness is built on two quarter-size canvases; outside the lights' bounding box it is copied exactly and cheaply, inside it is upscaled smoothly. About 1.7 ms with no lights, 3.5 ms with one point light, up to 8 ms with four lights that span the frame (the blit costs by pixel, so keep lights' reach modest). Call it once per draw.
  `L20.pass(ctx, { ambient: .2, t: lt, lights: [{ kind: 'cone', x: fl.x, y: fl.y, ang: fl.ang, spread: .6, len: 900, color: '#f4f8ff', flicker: { seed: 3, amt: .06, rate: 1.5 } }, { kind: 'point', x: 540, y: 900, r: 260, color: '#ffb25a' }] })`
- `L20.emergency(ctx, o)` SCREEN: the red-amber wash for the generator beat. `o = { level (0..1), t, pulse (0..1, default .15: a slow breathing of period 2.6 s, never a strobe) }`. A multiplied red cast (alpha .46 at level 1) and a red-black vignette. About 4 ms at full level. It darkens: keep the picture bright enough for LEGIBILITY L2 (mean luminance at least .20). Ramp `level` over at least .4 s.
- `L20.lamps(ctx, list, o)` small glowing LED candles / lamps (the glow only, no darkening) for lit scenes: `list = [{ x, y, r (default 60), color, alpha (default .8), core (default .6), flicker: { seed, amt, rate } }]`, `o = { t }`. Built on `FX18.glow` (it feeds the engine's bloom). About .4 ms a lamp.

## Traps
- THE TEXT GATE AND THE PASS. In the gate's mask pass (`TXA.mask`) `L20.pass` and `L20.emergency` return at once, like the engine's bloom and grade: a lighting veil is not a cover. Text you draw before the pass is darkened in the picture but NOT reported by the gate, so light your signs (a light on them) or draw hero text after the pass (`ep20_title.js` draws the plaque and the title after it). Snow, frost and sills are normal-blend white and DO count as covering text: keep them behind the text layer.
- Light is subtractive then additive: if your scene is bright and you want a night, set `ambient` honestly (.2 to .3), do not rely on `lift`. Every speaking face needs a light on it from something visible in the frame (LEGIBILITY L2). The pass is a screen-sized operation: do not call it twice a frame, and do not call it before the characters (it darkens what is already on the canvas). `ctx.clip()` before it restricts it (a split screen).
- PHOTOSENSITIVITY: do not animate a light's `power` or `ambient` across a full-frame luminance jump more than once a second; the blackout is one cascade of about 1.2 s and a ramp of at least .4 s.
- `SN20.snow` draws into the rectangle you give it (clip is on by default). For a full-frame blizzard use 0, 0, 1080, 1920 and `slice` to put the near layer in front. A flake count scales with area: a small pane needs `density` 3 to 6.
- Determinism: never feed `Date.now()` or a frame counter into `t`, `seed` or `depth`. Two instances of the same effect need different `seed`s.

## Testing (not loaded by index.html)
`wx20_demo.html` renders every function on a dark and on a pale background (open it with `?case=snowPale&t=1.2`). `node wx20_test.js list` names the cases; `sheet <case,...> <t,...> out.png [cellW]` writes a contact sheet to `build/ep20_wx/`; `one <case> <t>` one case at native size; `det` proves determinism (every case twice, in forward and reverse time order, in a second fresh page); `cost` times each case (rasterised: the canvas is read back after every draw, with and without the kit calls); `engine <case,...> <t,...>` draws cases through the real renderer (grade, bloom) over a real Strawberry; `enginedet` and `scenedet <scene,...>` repeat the determinism test through the real renderer (the real `title` and `end` scenes); `scenecost <scene,...>` times a real scene per frame.
