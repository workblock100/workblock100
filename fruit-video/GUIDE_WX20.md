# GUIDE_WX20: the Episode 20 weather and light kit (`ep20_fx.js`: `window.SN20` snow and cold, `window.L20` light)

Contract: `/tmp/shift_tools/briefs/WX20_API.md` (names and shapes are fixed; this file says what was built and the additions). Owner: the WX builder. It builds on `FX18` (`ep18_fx.js`, loads first): `FX18.rnd`, `FX18.noise`, `FX18.sprite`, `FX18.cone`, `FX18.glow`. Guard at DRAW time (`window.SN20 && SN20.snow`, `typeof L20.pass === 'function'`): the load order inside the episode is not yours. Delivery order: batch 1 `L20.pass`, `SN20.snow`, `depthAt`, `wind`; batch 2 `frost`, `sill`, `bank`, `cap`, `beam`, `breath`; batch 3 `spray`, `flurry`, `emergency`, `lamps`, `settle`, `maskPoints`. STATUS is marked on every function below. Demo and tests: `wx20_demo.html`, `wx20_test.js` (last section).

## Rules for every function
- Signature `SN20.name(ctx, o)` / `L20.name(ctx, o)`. WORLD functions draw in the transform you already have (the characters' transform after the scene camera). SCREEN functions (`L20.emergency`) reset the transform and use the whole canvas; call them from `over(lt)` (after the grade), never expect the camera to move them.
- PURE functions of their arguments and `t` (scene-local seconds, the `lt` the scene gets): no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no state that depends on how many frames were drawn. Randomness only from `FX18.rnd`. Same call twice, or frame t drawn first or last, gives identical pixels (tested).
- Each call saves and restores the context state. Soft shapes come from cached sprites; a frame is rendered up to 16 times for motion blur, so do not stack many big calls in a moving shot.
- Snow is in-world: behind or beside characters. NEVER over hero text, a face or the caption zone except as a light, small, low-alpha dressing (`fore: false` drops the big near layer; `slice: 'back'` / `'front'` split the field around your characters).
- Readability (LEGIBILITY L4): `tone: 'light'` (default) draws flakes white with a faint cool-blue rim and a tiny dark core shadow so they read on pale walls; `tone: 'dark'` draws plain white flakes with a soft glow for night skies and dark rooms. Nothing white on white: on a pale wall use `tone: 'light'` and keep `alpha` at .8 or more.

## The clock of the snow
- `SN20.SCENE_CLOCK = { forecast: 840, lot: 990, blackout: 1092, dayroom: 1240, night: 1379 }`: the clock minute (after midnight; 2:00 PM = 840) at the START of each scene. A scene's clock at local time `lt` is `SN20.SCENE_CLOCK.<id> + lt / 60` (the night's time-lapse runs 1379 to 1860: pass the lapse clock yourself).
- `SN20.depthAt(clockMinutes)` -> 0..1 accumulated snow depth (monotone cubic through 0 at 840, .25 at 990, .45 at 1212, .6 at 1360, .9 at 1860; 0 before 840, creeping to 1 after 1860). Use it for every sill, car and roof: `const d = SN20.depthAt(SN20.SCENE_CLOCK.dayroom + lt / 60); SN20.sill(ctx, { ..., depth: 10 + 60 * d })`. The same window is deeper at 8:40 than at 2:00.
- `SN20.wind(t, gusts)` -> -1..1 sideways push: a slow deterministic mix of two incommensurate sines plus the scripted gusts `[[t0, dur, amp], ...]` (a raised-cosine bump of height `amp` from t0 for dur seconds: a door opening, the plow; amp may be negative). `SN20.windInt(t, gusts)` -> px, the integral of the UNCLAMPED wind times 150 px/s (what `snow` uses for drift; keep the sum of base and gusts under 1). `SN20.GUST(t, gusts)` -> the gusts alone. The sound builder does not need these numbers (it follows the script's `doorgust` / `windgust` cues): pass a gust at each such cue so the picture and the sound agree.

## Snow and cold
- `SN20.snow(ctx, o)` [batch 1, landed] falling snow in a rectangle. `o = { x0, y0, x1, y1, t, seed, layers (1..4, default 3), density (1 = about 90 flakes per layer in a 1080 x 1920 rect; scales with area), fall (speed multiplier), wind (px/s sideways, default 0), gusts, sway (0..2, default 1), size (multiplier), alpha (multiplier), tone ('light' | 'dark'), fore (default true: the large soft near layer), slice ('all' | 'back' | 'front'), clip (default true: clip to the rectangle) }`. Layers back to front: far (tiny 2 to 3.6 px, dim, slow about 60 px/s, many), [mid-far when layers is 4], mid (4 to 7.5 px, about 120 px/s), near (9 to 14 px, big soft out of focus, about 190 px/s; only with layers 3 or more and `fore`). Each flake sways on two incommensurate frequencies; the whole field leans with `wind` + the wind curve (near flakes move 1.35x, far .5x: parallax). Flakes wrap in an invisible margin outside the rectangle: nothing pops. About 1.5 ms for 3 layers at density 1; cost grows with density x area. `slice: 'back'` draws far and mid (call it before the characters), `'front'` the near layer only (call it after them, only in shots where it crosses no face). Two fields need different `seed`s.
  `SN20.snow(ctx, { x0: 0, y0: 0, x1: 1080, y1: 1920, t: lt, seed: 3, density: 2, wind: 120, gusts: [[4.2, 1.2, .8]], tone: 'dark', slice: 'back' })`
- `SN20.frost(ctx, o)` [batch 2] frost on a window pane. `o = { x, y, w, h, amount (0..1), seed, t, tone }` (x, y = top-left). Branching ferns grow in from the edges and the bottom corners as `amount` grows; 0 draws nothing; 1 covers about 60 percent of the pane with white lace and a soft misted rim and leaves the centre clear; a faint glint travels across the crystals with `t`. Clip to the pane yourself if it is not a rectangle. `SN20.frost(ctx, { x: 300, y: 500, w: 480, h: 640, amount: SN20.depthAt(clock) * 1.4, seed: 2, t: lt })`
- `SN20.sill(ctx, o)` [batch 2] a snow heap on a ledge. `o = { x0, x1, y (the ledge top), depth (px), seed, tone }`. Lumpy profile that tapers at both ends, soft blue shadow under the lip, a few sparkles. Draw it after the ledge. `SN20.sill(ctx, { x0: 300, x1: 780, y: 1160, depth: 10 + 60 * d, seed: 4 })`
- `SN20.bank(ctx, o)` [batch 2] a drift or mound. `o = { x, y (base centre), w, h, seed, shade (0..1, blue shadow side, default .6), flip, tone }`.
- `SN20.cap(ctx, o)` [batch 2] snow lying along a flat top (car roof, sign, bench, hat brim). `o = { x0, x1, y (the top surface), depth, seed, round (0..1), tone }`. Draw AFTER the object; the layer sits above `y`.
- `SN20.beam(ctx, o)` [batch 2] a headlight or flashlight beam in falling snow. `o = { x, y, ang, spread, len, color, alpha, t, seed, flakes (default true), soft }`, built on `FX18.cone`; flakes inside the beam are brighter and sparkle.
- `SN20.breath(ctx, o)` [batch 2] a puff of cold breath. `o = { x, y, t, seed, period (1.9 s), dir (+1 right, -1 left), size, alpha, phase }`. Thicker and softer than steam with a faint darker edge so it reads on pale backgrounds.
- `SN20.spray(ctx, o)` [batch 3] snow thrown by a plow blade or a shovel. `o = { x, y, ang, t, t0, t1, seed, power, width, gravity }`.
- `SN20.flurry(ctx, o)` [batch 3] a burst of powder. `o = { x, y, r, t, t0, life, seed, n }`.
- `SN20.settle(ctx, o)` [batch 3] snow settling into a shape (the title). `o = { pts, from: { x, y, r }, t, t0, t1, seed, size, alpha }`; `SN20.maskPoints(drawFn, w, h, step, seed)` returns target points sampled from whatever `drawFn(g)` paints.

## Light: `L20.pass(ctx, o)` [batch 1, landed]
A lighting pass over the picture already drawn. Call it as the LAST world-space draw call of the scene's `draw` (camera transform active), or from `over()` with the same transform you used for the world. It darkens everything except what the lights reach, tints the dark, adds the lights' colour and optional bloom, grade and vignette. Positions are in the same units as the characters; the pass converts them through `ctx.getTransform()` (zoom, pan and roll all work).
- `o = { ambient (0..1: how much of the picture survives outside the lights; .22 dim but readable, 0 pitch black; never below .12 outside the pitch-black beat), tint (the colour of the dark, default '#0b1230'), lights: [...], bloom (0..1, default .5), grade ({ color, alpha, mode }: a final wash, mode 'multiply' | 'screen' | 'overlay' | 'soft-light' | 'lighter' | 'source-over'), vignette (0..1, default .25), lift (0..1: a floor under the darkness, default .08; the survival floor is max(ambient, lift)), t (scene-local seconds, drives the flicker; ADDED), glow (how much of each light's colour is added to the picture, default .32; ADDED) }`.
- Light kinds, each `{ kind, x, y, color, power (0..1.5, default 1; 0 is skipped), flicker: { seed, amt (clamped to .25), rate (Hz, clamped to 3) } }`:
  - `'point'`: `r` (radius px), `soft` (0..1, default .7). A lamp, a candle, a stopwatch, an LED.
  - `'cone'`: `ang` (rad, 0 = +x, pi/2 = down), `spread` (full angle rad), `len`, `soft`. A flashlight, a headlight, a downlight; a small pool lights the hand and the lens.
  - `'rect'`: `x, y` = the CENTRE, `w`, `h`, `soft`; `facing` (rad) optional spill direction, `spill` (px, default 1.4 x the larger side). A window, a TV, a laptop or clip screen. The rectangle itself is fully lit, with a soft halo beyond it.
  - `'strip'`: `x, y` to `x2, y2`, `width`, `soft`. A ceiling strip, an exit sign's wash.
- Returns the number of lights drawn. Cost: about 4 to 8 ms (two quarter-size canvases, one full-frame upscale for the darkness, one clipped upscale for the colour). Call it once per draw.
  `L20.pass(ctx, { ambient: .2, t: lt, lights: [{ kind: 'cone', x: fl.x, y: fl.y, ang: fl.ang, spread: .6, len: 900, color: '#f4f8ff', flicker: { seed: 3, amt: .06, rate: 1.5 } }, { kind: 'point', x: 540, y: 900, r: 260, color: '#ffb25a' }] })`
- `L20.emergency(ctx, o)` [batch 3] SCREEN: the red-amber wash for the generator beat. `o = { level (0..1), t, pulse (0..1, default .15: a slow breathing, never a strobe) }`.
- `L20.lamps(ctx, list, o)` [batch 3] small glowing LED candles / lamps (the glow only, no darkening) for lit scenes: `list = [{ x, y, r, color, flicker }]`.

## Traps
- Light is subtractive then additive: if your scene is bright and you want a night, set `ambient` honestly (.2 to .3), do not rely on `lift`. Every speaking face needs a light on it from something visible in the frame (LEGIBILITY L2).
- The pass is a SCREEN-sized operation: do not call it twice a frame; do not call it before the characters (it darkens what is already on the canvas).
- Do not animate a light's `power` across a full-frame luminance jump more than once a second (PHOTOSENSITIVITY): the blackout is one cascade of about 1.2 s and a ramp of at least .4 s.
- `SN20.snow` draws into the rectangle you give it; a window pane needs the pane's rectangle (clip is on by default). For a full-frame blizzard use 0, 0, 1080, 1920 and `slice` to put the near layer in front.
- Snow, frost and sills are normal-blend white: the text gate counts a normal-blend alpha .5 or more over hero text as covering it. Keep them behind the text layer.
- Determinism: never feed `Date.now()` or a frame counter into `t`, `seed` or `depth`.

## Testing (not loaded by index.html)
`wx20_demo.html` renders every function on a dark and on a pale background (open it with `?case=snow&t=1.2`). `node wx20_test.js sheet <case,...> <t,...> out.png [cellW]` writes a contact sheet to `build/ep20_wx/`; `node wx20_test.js det` proves determinism (every case twice, in forward and reverse time order, in a second fresh page); `node wx20_test.js cost` times each case; `node wx20_test.js list` names the cases.
