Contract: `/tmp/shift_tools/briefs/A21_API.md` (names and shapes fixed; anything below that is not in it is an ADDITION). Every function is a pure function of its arguments and `t` (no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no state); randomness only from `FX18.rnd`; text only through the UI kit, drawn live. Guard at DRAW time (`window.A21 && A21.kale`): the load order is not yours. Bench: `node a21_bench.js <view>...` writes `build/ep21_art/out/<view>.png` (views: `node a21_bench.js list`), `node a21_bench.js det` is the determinism check. ## Batch 1 (LANDED): Ms. Kale, Mr. Radish, the clipboard

### `A21.kale(o)` and `A21.radish(o)` -> a `drawChar` options object
Made like `A19.chef` / `A20.eggplant`: call, merge `x, y (feet), s`, draw with `drawChar`. **Pass `t` (scene time), `expr` and `pose` IN THE CALL** (the constructor reads them: an expression merged afterwards does not get its look-down or its pose). Kinds `kale` and `radish`; `who` `'kale'` / `'radish'` (the script's voice ids: the mouths move by themselves). `yaw` / `headYaw` work at every angle (front, three-quarter, profile, back): the leaf, the tuft, the jacket, the lanyard, the glasses all turn.
```js
drawChar(Object.assign(A21.kale({ t: lt, expr: 'kale_note', pose: 'write', cbTicks: 2.4 }), { x: 420, y: 1300, s: .9, yaw: .3 }));
drawChar(Object.assign(A21.radish({ t: lt, scanner: ss(lt, 3, 3.4) }), { x: 700, y: 1300, s: .9, yaw: -.3 }));
```
- **Ms. Kale**: a curly dark-green kale leaf (a ruffled crown and edge, a pale midrib), severe rectangular browline glasses, a navy blazer over a white blouse collar, the DOH lanyard and photo badge, a clipboard. Height at `s` 1: about 380 px (feet to the top of the curls); body centre `y - 210 s`. `expr`: `kale_neutral` (default), `kale_note` (eyes down), `kale_raise` (ONE eyebrow up: `raiseK` 0..1 animates it, default 1 for this expression; `raiseSide` +1 her right brow on screen right (default) / -1), `kale_satisfied` (the smallest smile). Her brows are drawn by the kit (`fp21_kale_brows`; the engine's are made transparent), so `browUp`, `face`, `expr` work as usual.
- **Mr. Radish**: a round red radish, white toward the bottom, a white root tip hanging between the legs (`root: false` hides it), a short tuft of leaves (`tuft: false`), a grey suit and navy tie, the same lanyard, a rugged tablet, SCANNER GLASSES: `scanner` 0..1 lights the lenses cyan with a moving scan line, corner ticks and a lit sensor on the right temple (bloom included). Height at `s` 1: about 330 px; body centre `y - 174 s`. `expr`: `radish_flat` (default), `radish_note` (eyes down), `radish_impressed` (brows up 2 px: the most he moves), `radish_buried`: the constructor hides the legs and the shadow, raises his right arm straight up and knocks the glasses askew: put `y` where his body centre should be plus `.9 * ry * s` (about `y = centreY + 94 s`) and draw the binder pile AFTER him, below his face (his face must stay clear: the audit's face box is `A21.faceBox(o)`).
- **Poses** (`pose`): Kale `'clip'` (default: the clipboard against her left side, `cbPen: true` puts a pen in the right hand), `'write'` (the board in front, the pen hand writing with a small scribble motion; `penWrite: false` stops it), `'rest'`, `'free'` (nothing set: give your own `arms`, `grip`, `hold`). Radish `'tablet'` (default), `'tap'` (tablet in front, right index finger tapping it), `'clip'`, `'write'`, `'rest'`, `'free'`. Held poses use `gripSpace: 'body'` so the hands stay with the body as it turns. Any key you pass (`arms`, `grip`, `hold`, `look`, ...) overrides the pose.
- Options for both: `glasses: false`, `badge21: false` (no lanyard), `badgeText: false` (no lettering on the badge; the held poses that cover it set this), `badgeName` ('KALE' / 'RADISH'), `suit21: false` (no jacket) or `suit21: { color, lapel, shirt, tie, collar, hem, ys, neck, pocket, button }`, `frill: false` (Kale, no curly edge).
- `A21.centre(o)` -> `[x, y]` the body centre and `A21.faceBox(o)` -> `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` the audit's face box (front view), both in the current canvas coordinates, after you merged `x, y, s`: frame close-ups with them, keep props, piles and HUD off the face box.

### The clipboard: held prop `fp21_clipboard`, set piece `A21.clipboard(ctx, o)`
- Held (`hold: { l: 'fp21_clipboard' }`, the poses do it): `cbTicks` 0..n (rows ticked; a fraction draws the last tick stroke on, so `cbTicks: lerp(0, 3, u)` animates three ticks), `cbItems` (default `FRONT DOOR, STAFFING, LABELS, MED PASS, BREAKS`), `cbFail` (a row index: a red X; `cbFailK` 0..1 draws it), `cbHeader` ('DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH'), `cbSub` ('SURVEY CHECKLIST'), `cbScale` (1 = 94 x 124 body px), `cbRot`. The hand grips the board's outer edge; the rows keep a margin on that side so the fist never covers the lettering. Lettering is UI-kit dressing (`hero: false`), drawn only when it is 7.5 px or more on screen (grey bars below that). `PROP_BOX` is mirrored and stays below the owner's face box in every pose.
- Set piece: `A21.clipboard(ctx, { x, y (centre), w (default 600), h, rot, items, ticks, fail, failK, header, sub, hero (default TRUE), ink, rowAlpha [per row 0..1], shadow }) -> { box, rows: [{ cx, cy, box (the check box), text: [x, y] (left end of the item, cap-band centre), size, local }], sheet, clip, w, h, rot }` (current coordinates, rotation included). The title card uses it.
- `fp21_pen` (held: a navy ballpoint, tip down, `penAng`), `fp21_tablet` (held: rugged case, lit screen, `tabLit` 0..1, `tabRows`, `tabTick` (rows checked, fractional), `tabScale`).

### Accessories registered (for `acc` lists of other characters too)
`fp21_badge` (the lanyard and photo badge: works on any kind; `badge21: { cw, drop, bx, photo }`), `fp21_suit` (any kind; `suit21`), `fp21_kale_frill`, `fp21_kale_brows`, `fp21_kale_glasses`, `fp21_scanner` (`scanner`, `glassTilt`). All are yaw-aware (`V2C.YAW_ACC`).

### Traps
- Put `expr`, `pose` and `t` in the constructor call, not in a later `Object.assign` (the look-down of `kale_note` / `radish_note` and the poses are set there).
- The surveyors' faces are big for their bodies: anything you hold in front of them must stay below `A21.faceBox(o)[3]`. The kit's own props do.
- `radish_buried` has no legs: `y` is not the feet. Draw the pile after him, below the face box.
- Kale's frill sticks out about 22 px (times `s`) past her silhouette at the crown: give her that much air from the frame edge.

## Batch 2 (LANDED): H21, the HUD and split-screen kit (`ep21_hud.js`)
Line widths and label sizes are in SCREEN pixels whatever the transform (a reticle drawn inside a zoomed camera keeps its weight). HUD strokes have a dark under-stroke, so cyan reads on pale walls too. Cost per call (headless, canvas flushed): reticle 2.3 ms, scan 1.5, hudFrame 3.6, stamp .8, split 2.3 plus your two halves, track .01.

### `H21.reticle(ctx, o)` -> `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` (the bracket box, current units)
`o = { x, y (the CENTRE of the target), w, h (its box; omit both for a point: 110 screen px), t, t0, lock (0..1), color (H21.CYAN default, H21.RED for a deficiency), label, sub (a second line under the box), alpha, labelPx (32), lw (3), hero, id, flash (true), lockDur (.55) }`. With `t0` and no `lock`, it locks by itself over `lockDur` (brackets close in with a small overshoot, the dashed ring tightens and goes solid, the centre cross appears, ONE pulse ring when it locks); pass `lock` to drive it yourself. The tag types in over the last half of the lock (a block cursor while it types); it is hero text once typed (`labelPx` >= 30), so keep it 40 px from the frame edges and out of the caption zone. WORLD: call it inside your camera transform with the object's world box and it follows the object through every camera move; SCREEN: call it in `over()`.
```js
const tr = H21.track({ id: 'closet', boxAt: tt => closetBox(tt) }, lt);          /* closetBox(tt): the door's world box at any time (your own motion function) */
H21.reticle(ctx, Object.assign(tr, { t: lt, t0: CUE.lockon, label: 'OCCUPIED', color: H21.RED }));
```
### `H21.track(obj, t)` -> `{ x, y, w, h, box }`
`obj = { id, boxAt (t -> [x0, y0, x1, y1]) or box, lag (.08 s), smooth (.1 s), jitter (1.2 screen px) }`. With `boxAt` the box trails the object by `lag` and is averaged over `smooth` (a pure function of `t`: no state); with a plain `box` you only get the small hunting jitter. Feed the result straight into `reticle`.
### `H21.scan(ctx, o)` SCREEN -> `{ pos, u }` or null
`o = { t, t0, dur (1.2), dir ('down' | 'up' | 'right' | 'left'), color, alpha, band (wake, 300 px), grid (true) }`: a hot line with a fading wake and a faint grid inside the wake, screen-blended (it brightens, never covers); in the caption zone (y 1380 to 1700) it is drawn at 22 percent (light haze). Nothing before `t0`, fades out after `t0 + dur`. `pos` is the line's current y (or x): light things up as it passes. Call it in `over()`.
### `H21.hudFrame(ctx, o)` SCREEN -> `{ inner, chip }`
`o = { t, mode ('scan' | 'lock' | 'deficiency'), count, countText ('DEFICIENCIES: '), text (the chip's string), alpha, color, chyron (true), wave (true), top, bottom }`: four corner brackets (top 262 with the clock pill shown, 70 with `chyron: false`; bottom 1352, above the captions), a ruler, a faint lens tint, the mode chip at the top right (`SCANNING`, `TARGET LOCKED`, `DEFICIENCY FOUND` in white on red) with a 1 Hz dot, the count under it, a tiny waveform at the bottom left. Hero text: draw nothing over the top-right corner (x 600 to 1040, y 270 to 400).
### `H21.split(ctx, o, drawLeft, drawRight)` SCREEN -> `{ L, R, xAt }`
`o = { t, x (the divider's x at mid height, 540; animate it freely), angle (tilt, radians), gap (14), labelL, labelR, colorL ('#2456b8'), colorR ('#e0542e'), labelY (300), labelPx (34), labelAlpha, faceCut ('error' | 'off') }`. Each half is clipped exactly (no bleed) and drawn by your function `draw(ctx, box)`, `box = { x0, y0, x1, y1, cx, cy, w, h, poly, side, xAt(y) }`; set the half's camera inside with `H21.halfCam(ctx, box, worldX, worldY, zoom, roll)` (it puts that world point at the centre of the half). Then the divider (a white band, a thin edge in each half's colour, a soft shadow on both sides) and the two label chips, each wholly inside its half (shrunk to fit; skipped if the half is under 300 px wide). Call it from `draw()` with the global `ctx`.
- For the audit, everything a half registers (bodies, faces, props) is cut to the part of the screen that half shows, so the two halves never "cover" each other; a FACE cut by the divider by more than 10 percent is reported (`face:x is cut by the split-screen divider`): keep faces 30 px clear of it (`faceCut: 'off'` for a deliberate wipe).
- The text gate sees both halves' text (it is drawn live in each half); text under the divider band or cut by a half's edge is reported: keep text 30 px from the divider. Draw nothing over the label chips after the call.
```js
draw(lt) { H21.split(ctx, { t: lt, x: lerp(1100, 540, ez(lt, 0, .6)), angle: .05, labelL: 'WITH THE STATE', labelR: 'A NORMAL NIGHT' },
  (c, B) => { H21.halfCam(c, B, 540, 1000, .9); drawStateSide(lt); }, (c, B) => { H21.halfCam(c, B, 540, 1000, .9); drawNormalSide(lt); }); }
```
### `H21.stamp(ctx, o)` -> `{ x0, y0, x1, y1, cx, cy, w, h, landed, k }`
`o = { x, y (centre), s (1), rot (-.12; clamped to +-.2 unless tilt), text ('SURVEYED'), t, t0 (the impact: snap it to the stamp cue's frame), color ('#d3203a'), size (72 before s), alpha (.94), hero, id, ring (true), font ('Bangers'), pre (.14) }`: nothing before `t0 - pre`; it drops in from 1.9x, lands on `t0` with a squash that rings out in about .25 s, an ink ring spreads and fades, a few specks of ink stay; mottled ink (a pattern fill, so the text gate still reads the letters). The text is hero once it has landed and is 30 px or more on screen.

## Batch 3 (LANDED): the survey props (in `ep21_art.js`)
Every prop draws in the CURRENT transform (inside your camera, or in `over()`), saves and restores the context and returns its axis-aligned box `[x0, y0, x1, y1]` (current units). `x, y, s, rot` place it. Lettering is UI-kit dressing (`hero: false`; pass `hero: true` for a close insert where the viewer must read it) and is skipped where it would be under 8 px on screen. Cost per call (headless, flushed): label .1 ms, padlock .14, sanitizer .65, fridgeThermo .7, crashCart 1.5, binder .13 (40 tumbling binders 6.5), policyCloset 2.2 closed, 5.3 open and full.
- `A21.label(ctx, { x, y (centre), s (1 = 250 x 82), rot, text ('OPENED 11/6 3:04P SB'; wraps to two lines and shrinks to fit), t, t0 (slapped on: nothing before t0, then a quick pop), peel (0..1: the right end curls back, the lettering fades by .2, at .6 to 1 it lifts off and fades), band ('#e8542e'), w, h, hero })`: the white survey label. For "the labels peel off", animate `peel`.
- `A21.padlock(ctx, { x, y (body centre), s (1 = 70 x 60 body), rot, t, open (0..1: the shackle lifts and swings), t0 (snapped shut: a small bounce), color ('brass' | 'steel' | '#hex') })`.
- `A21.sanitizer(ctx, { x, y (centre), s (1 = 128 x 226), t, pump (0..1: the push bar goes in, a dollop of foam grows under the nozzle), level (0..1 the gel window), t0 (refilled: the level rises over .5 s), label ('SANITIZER' | false) })`: a wall dispenser.
- `A21.fridgeThermo(ctx, { x, y (dial centre), s (1 = 160 px dial), deg (F, default 38), t, wobble (0..1 the needle shivers), hero })`: a dial thermometer on its hook, -20 to 80 F, a green SAFE band 33 to 41, red above.
- `A21.crashCart(ctx, { x, y (floor centre), s (1 = 300 wide, about 560 tall with the backboard), t, open (0..1: the top drawer slides out, the seal breaks), restock (0..1: supplies pop into the open drawer), sealed (true: a numbered red breakaway seal), seal ('0042137'), t0 (re-sealed: the tag swings) })`: front view with a defibrillator, an oxygen tank and the backboard.
- `A21.binder(ctx, { x, y (CENTRE), w (60), h (80), rot, color, spine (label text, false: none), view ('auto' | 'cover' | 'spine'; auto = spine when w < .45 h), seed (picks a colour from A21.BINDER_COLORS), hero, grp })`: ONE policy binder that fills exactly its `w x h` body rectangle: use it for every physics body. Its label is drawn only while the binder is within about 20 degrees of upright ON SCREEN (camera roll included) and fades out past that, so a tumbling binder never shows tilted or upside-down text; labels share the group `fp21_binder` (piled binders may overlap each other's labels).
- `A21.policyCloset(ctx, { x, y (floor centre), s (1 = 380 x 700), t, open (0..1: the doors swing out to about 95 degrees), packed (0..1: the share of binders still on the shelves; each slot has a fixed random `rank`, a slot is drawn while rank < packed), strain (0..1: the closed doors tremble and bow, a red binder corner and papers poke out of the seam), label ('POLICIES' | false), hero }) -> { box, inside, shelves: [{ y, x0, x1, slots: [{ x, y, w, h, color, spine, rank, shelf, i, rot }] }], seam }` (current coordinates). The avalanche: when `open` passes your cue, hand each slot whose rank is above the falling `packed` to the physics as a body with the slot's `x, y, w, h, color, spine` (draw it with `A21.binder(..., view: 'spine')` until it tips, then any view): it starts exactly where the closet drew it. The spines read `4.1` ... `8.x` (shelf 4 is 7.x: policy 7.4 is `shelves[3].slots[3]`).
- `A21.agencyBadge(o)` -> a copy of the options with the accessory `fp21_agency` added (a white AGENCY sticker with a purple band on the left chest; a decal, so it turns with `yaw`). `drawChar(Object.assign(A21.agencyBadge(A11.straw({ t })), { x, y, s }))`. `A21.AGENCY` is the accessory name.

## Determinism and the bench
`node a21_bench.js det` draws every bench view twice in one page and once in a second page and compares pixels, with `Math.random` and `Date.now` trapped while drawing: all SAME. `node a21_bench.js cost` prints the cost per call (canvas flushed per call, so the numbers are honest; without the flush Chrome's deferred canvas charges one call's pixels to the next).
