## Scene `title` (5.2 s, no chyron, no scrim: set `noChyron: true, noScrim: true`)
Beat `logo` 0.2 to 5.0. Sound cues you can sync to (offsets from 0.2): `vaultdial` 0.0 (a combination dial ratcheting and slowing down over 2.4 s), `clunk` 2.4 (the dial locks, bolts shoot), `textding` 2.5 (the lettering lands), `theme7` 0.0 (the series sting; its landing is at about 2.4).
Concept: THE FLOAT POOL as a heist-film title. A huge steel combination dial (knurled rim, tick marks and numbers 0 to 95 round the edge, a red index mark at the top, engraved) fills the middle of the frame in a dark teal room with a hard spotlight and haze; it spins and slows with the ratchet (one visible tick per click), locks at 2.4 with a flash and a ring of bolts withdrawing; the dial then drifts up and out of the way or dissolves into the lettering (your call) and the title lands: "SHIFT HAPPENS" (series style), an "EPISODE 13" pill, "THE FLOAT POOL" (big, condensed, gold shine sweeping across), a small tagline line such as "Every nurse deserves thirty minutes." All through `UI.stack`, perfectly centred, nothing within 40 px of the edge. Silhouettes of the six crew can stand in the haze behind the dial, evenly spaced on one floor line (optional). New idea required: do not copy the Episode 12 projector.
