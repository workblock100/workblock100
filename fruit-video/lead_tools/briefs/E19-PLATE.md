## What this scene is
A single, unbroken camera move, 23 seconds, with NO cuts: the plate travels from the kitchen pass-through to Strawberry on her med pass through five spaces, and the camera is a dolly that follows it with the parallax of a real set. The viewer must feel it is one take: layered depth (foreground props passing, background rooms sliding), characters turning to track the plate, hand-offs in the frame, the plate changing as people add to it. This is the series' first one-take shot: make it feel like one.

## The path (a long world strip, left to right)
Kitchen pass-through (the window and a tray rail) -> corridor A -> the nurses' station -> hall B with Mr. Pear in his wheelchair by a window -> corridor C -> the med cart where Strawberry stands, with a wall clock above her. The camera dollies right at a speed that varies (it slows for each speaker so the speaker settles near the middle of the frame, it speeds up in the `walk` beats), with gentle vertical and rotational drift; 2.5D depth: three to four layers moving at different speeds (the rail, the wall, the far wall, the doors beyond), floors with reflections. Characters in three-quarter and profile views (`yaw`), the walkers' legs actually walking (footsteps will be detected by the animation pass).
## The plate (a held prop, `F19.dish(ctx, 'plate', { items, stickers })` or your own)
Contents: the beige turkey mold, the one roll, the beige pie, a cup of coffee pudding. It changes as it travels: Lemon sticks a yellow note HOLD on it (`peel` 5.04), Tomato a round FUN COMMITTEE sticker (`peel` 7.65), Onion an allergen label CONTAINS: BREAD and stabs a probe thermometer into the turkey, readout 78 F (`probebeep` 10.60), Binder clips himself onto the rim with a PENDING tag, and Mr. Pear peers at it. The hand-offs are visible flights between hands (a short arc, a catch with a bounce of the plate), never a cut.

## `tools_plate_path.js` (REQUIRED for the sound)
A node script (no DOM) that writes `build/ep19/plate_path.json`: an array sampled at 10 Hz over the scene, ABSOLUTE seconds (`scene start 75.330 + local`): `{ "t": ..., "x": <the camera's world x>, "room": "kitchen" | "corridor" | "station" | "hall" | "medroom", "speakerX": <world x of whoever is speaking, or null> }`. The picture reads the same camera function (put the camera path in a DOM-free module `ep19_plate_sim.js` exporting `build()`/`camAt(lt)` used by both) so the sound's room tone and panning follow what the camera shows. Run it when your path is final and again after any change; tell the lead.
