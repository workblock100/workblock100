## Sound effects: the work
Refine every new effect so it sits well in the mix and sounds like what it is (measure with RMS, peak and a spectrogram; you cannot listen, so say so). Add anything the cue list implies but lacks.

## The score: `build_music13.py`
Write `score(script, scenes, lines, N)` returning a (2, N) stereo bed, like `build_music12.py` (import what you need from `build_music6.py` to `build_music12.py`; the title and end reuse `cue_title7` / `cue_end7` from `build_music7.py`). The heist is a cool, funky caper that turns tender at the end. Mood per scene:
- title: the series sting (cue_title7) plus a vault-dial pulse: a muted click-clack that slows with the dial and a low sub swell; hit on the clunk at 2.4.
- open: dry, late-night noir funk: a slow upright-bass walk, brushed snare, a muted trumpet, a vibraphone; very quiet under the dialogue; the schedule insert holds a single pedal tone; a record scratch ends the music dead at `end`.
- plan: a cool heist groove (vibraphone, walking bass, finger snaps, a mallet motif) that builds a layer per obstacle; a held, tense drone with a heartbeat tick under the Q-word beat; a brass stab on "mandatory in-service"; the hologram collapse takes the music out.
- crew: a punchy funk groove (about 104 bpm: clavinet-style stabs, bass, tight drums) that restarts with a hit on every card slam (one stab per card, the same chord family); a reverse cymbal into each freeze; a spacey chord for the Moon card.
- hall: tension: a sub drone, a slow pulse, strings on harmonics; at `lasers` (slow motion) everything pitches down and stretches (a heartbeat, a long reversed swell), then snaps back into time at the end of the beat with a riser into the sting.
- cross: an upbeat caper groove (swing feel, walking bass, brushed snare, brass stabs, a jazz organ), tighter and busier; a stab on every cut; the slip gets a slide whistle fall.
- vault: eerie first (wind, a drone, a lonely music box), a dissonant stab when the beams find Brenda; then the heart: from "I've got it. Go." a tender string line (violin, cello, harp) that carries through Brenda's exit and Strawberry sitting; a soft piano as she takes her bite; then SILENCE from the Moon's line until the freeze; the red crash (percussion, alarm, a brass hit) for the call-light cascade; thin, quick, comic under the crew running out; and a single warm note under "Pending."; the sting and the door close.
- end: cue_end7's reprise (the series theme).
Quality bar: this episode is meant to be a leap in polish. Layer real arrangements (bass, drums, harmony, a lead) rather than a loop; keep dynamics and stereo width; make each scene's music end where its visual ends.
