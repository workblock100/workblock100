## What the sheet is
Eight scenes of the episode show a spreadsheet application ("GridSheet", invented; never imitate a real product's logo, icon set, name or clip art) either full screen (the title, inserts, the end card), on the rolling TV of the all-hands (a laggy video call with a shared screen) or on a laptop at the nurses' station. Builders of the scenes need ONE kit that draws it, animates every operation the script performs, and passes the text gate. The sheet is "the model": cells hold the staff, rooms and policies; the operations (Merge & Center, Insert Row, Remove Duplicates, Fill Down, a lookup that fails, Find & Replace, Undo, Save As) are what the episode is about. The look is in CONTEXT15 (palette: white `#ffffff`, grid `#d9dce1`, header grey `#f1f3f5`, text `#22262b`, selection blue `#2b6cff`, accent green `#1e8e5a`, amber `#ffbf1f`, error red `#d93025`, dialog grey `#eef0f3`).

## API (design it, document it in `GUIDE_SHEET.md`, keep each call a pure function of `t`/`u` and draw text only through `UI`)
All draw into the current `ctx` in a local frame whose origin is the sheet window's top-left (the caller translates/scales: the sheet must look right at any scale from 0.35 (on a TV) to 3 (a close-up of a cell); text sizes are in sheet units; `hero` is true only for what the viewer must read, everything else `hero: false`; when the sheet is small on a TV, set a `dressing: true` option that marks ALL its text `hero: false, crop: true` so the gate only notes it).
1. `SH15.sheet(o, t)`: the whole window: title bar (document name e.g. `SYNERGY_MODEL_v47_FINAL_REAL`, the app name GridSheet with an original logo (a green rounded square with a 2x2 grid)), ribbon (tabs: Home Insert Data Review View; a row of buttons with ORIGINAL icons drawn as vector shapes: Merge & Center (two boxes with converging arrows), Find & Replace (magnifier), Remove Duplicates (stacked rows with a cross), Fill (a small square drag handle), Sort, Undo/Redo arrows, a Save icon), formula bar (name box `A1` + `fx` + content), column letters and row numbers, the grid with cell text (left/right/centre alignment, bold, fills, borders, number formats), merged-cell regions, frozen header row, scroll offset, zoom, sheet tabs at the bottom (e.g. `Sheet1`, `Staff`, `Policies`, `Sheet16`), a status bar (`Ready`, `Sum: 31`). Options: `cells: { 'A1': { v, align, bold, fill, color, size, fmt } }`, `cols`, `rows`, `sel: 'A1:C1'` (blue outline, light fill, a fill handle square at the bottom-right corner), `active: 'A1'`, `ribbon: { tab: 'Home', hot: 'merge', press: 0..1 }` (a button highlighted and pressed), `formula: '=SUM(B2:B30)'`, `scroll: [x, y]`, `tabs`, `status`.
2. `SH15.pointer(x, y, { kind: 'arrow'|'hand'|'cross'|'text', press: 0..1 })`: an original mouse pointer (not the OS shape: a chunkier friendly one with a white outline), a press ring, optionally a drag trail.
3. `SH15.dialog(o, u)`: a modal over a dimmed sheet: `kind: 'warn'` (an amber triangle, "Merging keeps only the top-left value. Other values will be discarded." [OK] [Cancel]; the wording is ours, not a real product's), `'info'`, `'error'`, `'findReplace'` (two text boxes Find what / Replace with, buttons Find Next, Replace, Replace All, Close, an options row; typed text animates with a caret: `typed: [fromString, progress]`), `'saveAs'` (file name field, a folder list, Save / Cancel), `'cantUndo'` ("Can't undo." [OK]), `'removeDuplicates'` (a checklist of columns, "12 duplicate values found and removed. 28 unique values remain."), with a pop-in (`u` 0..1: scale and fade), a title bar, focus ring on the default button and `press` on a button. Everything legible at phone size when the dialog is the subject (dialog text is hero, at least 30 px on screen when it is a full-screen insert).
4. `SH15.merge(o, u)`: THE signature animation, used by the title and the announcement: a block of cells (for example `A1:C1`, or two big cells on a TV) whose texts, borders and fills merge into one: borders and gridlines between them dissolve, the selected region's outline tightens, the discarded values fade and fall away (physically: they drop and spin out of the cell with a small shake; use your own simple animation, no physics library needed), the surviving top-left value glides to the centre and scales up to its final size, a chime-ready flash on the final frame. `u` 0..1, parameterised by the region and the texts; it must be exact on the final frame (the merged cell is perfectly centred, to the pixel; verify by measurement).
5. `SH15.flipText(from, to, u, o)`: letter by letter text change (BREAK becomes PENDING, 5 letters to 7 letters): an odometer / split-flap style where each letter slides out upward and the new letter slides in from below inside a clip, with a stagger, in the sheet font or in the sign font (Bangers etc.) at any size; the string widths animate smoothly (the centre stays centred); it must be usable for world signs (the BREAK ROOM sign on a door: `UI.sign` with the new text) and cells. Transitional frames are `hero: false, crop: true` (they are allowed to be cut by the clip), the first and last frames are exact and hero.
6. `SH15.errorCell(ref, text, u)`: a red error value (`#N/A`, `#REF!`, `#VALUE!`) with the little corner marker and a floating tooltip ("Value not found in table"), a shake on appearance.
7. `SH15.fillDrag(o, u)`: the fill handle being dragged down: a dashed outline stretching over cells, a tooltip with the count, the copies appearing in the cells with a ripple. `SH15.removeDuplicates(o, u)`: rows highlighted amber then struck through then collapsing (the rows below slide up). `SH15.insertRow(o, u)`: a new blue row sliding in, rows below shifting down by one row height.
8. `SH15.call(o, t)`: a laggy video-call window for the rolling TV and the laptop: participant tile with a name label and a mic icon, a frozen state (frozen frame + spinner + "Connection unstable"), a screen-share state (the sheet shown inside, with a small speaker thumbnail in a corner), a chat panel (messages that arrive: `chat: [{ who, text, t0 }]`), a hand-raise icon, a "You are muted" banner. All drawn so that on the TV (scale 0.3 to 0.6) it is dressing, and in an insert (scale 1 to 2) it is hero.
9. `SH15.binder(o, t)`: Binder's chat bubble (the assistant's speech box): the tail toward the mascot, text with a typewriter reveal (`typed`), two buttons ("Yes please", "No thanks"): "No thanks" is slightly greyed and runs away from the pointer (`runaway: u`); a small helper (the mascot itself is drawn by E15-ART's `A15.binder` through `drawChar`, you only draw the bubble and position hooks).
10. `SH15.cursorPath(points, t)`: helper that moves the pointer along eased waypoints with natural overshoot and clicks (`click: [t0, t1]`), returns `{ x, y, press }`.
11. Layout helpers: `SH15.cellRect(ref, o)` returns the sheet-local rect of a cell or range (so the scene can aim the camera at a cell, point the pointer, or attach a world effect to a cell), `SH15.colX`, `SH15.rowY`.
Document the cost: a frame of the full sheet must draw in under about 40 ms.
