# A20_API: the art kit contract for Episode 20 (`ep20_art.js`: `window.A20`, owner LOT; `ep20_props.js`: `window.P20`, owner BLACKOUT). Written by the lead; used by every scene builder

Everybody codes against THIS file at once. Names, argument shapes and units are FIXED; the owners may ADD (document at the top of the file and in `GUIDE_ART20.md`), never rename or reshape. Until a function lands, draw a stand-in and swap in the real one when it exists (check at DRAW time). Conventions as `F19_API.md`/`WX20_API.md`: pure functions of their arguments and `t`, `ctx` state saved and restored, randomness only from `FX18.rnd`, cached sprites for anything static, text only through the UI kit. How characters are built and extended: `GUIDE_ART15.md`, `GUIDE_TURN.md`, and `GUIDE.md` lines on `EXT.acc.<name> = (o, k, t, layer)` (accessories; draw only when `layer === 'front'`) and `EXT.prop.<name> = (side, o, t)` (held props) with `PROP_BOX` entries for the audit. Names carry the prefix `fp20_`.

## A20 (owner LOT): winter gear and two new characters
- `A20.winter(o, g)` returns `o` (a `drawChar` options object from any constructor: `A6`..`A15`, `A7`) with the gear `g` added to its `acc` list. `g = { hat ('beanie' | 'earflap' | 'hood' | 'none', default 'beanie'), hatColor, pom (true: a pom-pom), scarf (true), scarfColor, mittens (true), boots (true), coat (false by default; true only if it holds in every pose), wind (-1..1: the scarf tail flaps, the pom-pom leans), snow (0..1: a dusting on the hat, shoulders and scarf), shiver (0..1: a fast tiny tremble of the whole figure, applied as a position jitter by the caller via the returned `o.shiverDx/Dy`), breath (true: adds `SN20.breath` at the mouth automatically) }`. Faces stay visible: the hat sits above the brows, the scarf below the mouth's lowest point (the mouth moves while it speaks), mittens and boots never cover a face box. It must look right for Strawberry, Mango, Lemon, Onion, Tomato and Binder (a tiny scarf), in front, three-quarter (`yaw`) and profile views. Default colours per `who`: straw red beanie with a white pom, mango orange, lemon yellow with a navy scarf, onion cream with a green scarf, tom a pompom hat in HR purple.
- `A20.turnip(o)` (`who: 'turnip'`): TURNIP, the snow-plow driver: a white-and-purple turnip with a short green leafy top poking out of a trucker cap with ear flaps, a hi-vis orange vest with reflective stripes over a work jacket, heavy brows, a flat, unbothered face (`expr` `turnip_flat` default, `turnip_deadpan`, `turnip_proud`). Props: a travel mug (`fp20_mug`), gloves. Seen mostly in the cab window of the plow, close, and once from behind.
- `A20.eggplant(o)` (`who: 'egg'`): EGGPLANT, the 11-to-7 nurse: a glossy deep-purple eggplant with a green calyx cap, scrubs top with a lanyard, tired kind eyes, a scarf and a hat (she is in her car). `expr` `egg_stuck` (default), `egg_calm`, `egg_cosy`. Props: a phone (`fp20_phone`, `phoneLit`), a pizza slice (`fp20_slice`, `bite` 0..1).
- `A20.ditchPicture(ctx, o)` -> draws the picture of Eggplant in her small car, nose down in a snowy ditch at night, hazard lights blinking slowly (1 Hz, dim), eating pizza from a box on the dashboard, content: `o = { x, y (centre), w, h, t, eat (0..1: how much pizza is gone), phoneRing (0/1: she answers the phone), frame (true: a rounded border with a faint video-call look; the text on it goes through the UI kit) }`. Used by the LOT scene (as the phone call's picture-in-picture, `eat` 0) and the END card (`eat` 1, happy). Returns its box.
- `A20.car(ctx, o)`: a small hatchback seen from the side: `o = { x, y (ground centre), s, color, snow (0..1: 0 clean; .35 a cap of snow on the roof, hood and trunk; .7 a rounded mound with the windows buried; 1 a smooth HILL with only a mirror and the antenna showing), facing (+1/-1), lights (headlights on, with a beam via SN20.beam if present), seed, t }`. Returns its box. `snow` is animated by the scene (the plow raises it from .35 to .9 in a second).
- `A20.plowTruck(ctx, o)`: a pickup with a plow blade, side view: `o = { x, y, s, facing, t, blade (the blade's angle), load (0..1: snow heaped in front of the blade), beacon (true: an amber light on the roof sweeping a slow glow, at most 1 pulse per second), headlights (true), cab (true: draws the cab window as an empty rectangle and returns it so the scene can draw Turnip inside via `A20.turnip`) }`. Returns `{ box, cab: [x0, y0, x1, y1], blade: [x, y], exhaust: [x, y] }`. The scene adds `SN20.spray` at `blade` and `FX18.smoke` at `exhaust`.
- `A20.snowman(ctx, o)`: `o = { x, y, s, build (0..1: three stacked balls rising, then the face and arms), lean, hat ('party' | 'none'), nose ('carrot'), t, seed }`; a charming lopsided snowman with twig arms and coal eyes; a party hat when Tomato built it.
- `fp20_shovel` held prop (`hold: { r: 'fp20_shovel' }`): a snow shovel; options `shovelLoad` (0..1 snow on the blade), `shovelAng`. `A20.shovelThrow(o, t, t0)` returns the blade's tip `{x, y, ang}` over a dig-and-throw cycle so the scene can attach `SN20.spray`.

## P20 (owner BLACKOUT): the shared props (`ep20_props.js`), delivered FIRST (within 30 minutes) because NIGHT and FORECAST use them
- `fp20_flashlight` held prop (`flOn` 0/1, `flAng` rad): a chunky flashlight; `P20.flashlightPose(o, t)` returns `{ x, y, ang }` (the lens position and the beam's direction in world units) so the scene adds an `L20` cone and `SN20.beam` there.
- `P20.ledCandle(ctx, o)`: a battery tea light or pillar: `o = { x, y, s, on (0..1), t, seed }` draws the candle and its tiny warm glow (the scene adds the L20 point light); flicker is slow and soft (never a strobe).
- `fp20_stopwatch` held prop: `swTime` (seconds, shown as M:SS.s through the UI kit, dressing text; at rest and close it is hero text, keep clear of snow and glow); a green-blue LCD glow.
- `P20.outlet(ctx, o)`: a wall plate with two sockets: `o = { x, y, s, color ('white' | 'red'), plug (a cord plugged in: 'coffee' | 'oxygen' | 'phone' | null), label (RED = EMERGENCY, UI kit), t }`.
- `P20.coffeeMaker(ctx, o)`: a drip coffee maker with a glass pot and a 12:00 display: `o = { x, y, s, power (0/1), display ('12:00' | '--:--' | ''), pot (0..1), sad (0..1: the little status LED dies), t }`.
- `P20.cot(ctx, o)`: a folding camp cot with a grey blanket and a pillow: `o = { x, y, s, sleeper (null | 'mango' | 'lemon' | 'tom' | 'onion': draws the sleeping character under the blanket), zzz (0/1), t }`.
- `P20.exitSign(ctx, o)`: a green running-man exit sign with a soft green glow; `P20.emergencyLamp(ctx, o)`: a twin-head battery emergency light on a wall: `o = { x, y, s, on (0..1) }` (the scene adds the L20 cones).
- `P20.moon(ctx, o)`: reuse the series' Moon (grep `moon` in `ep13_art.js` / `ep12_art.js`) in a window pane with a sky and optional clouds: `o = { x, y, r, t, cloud (0..1: how much of him is behind the weather; 1 = only a glow), rise (-1..1: where he is on his arc), expr }`. Used by DAYROOM (cloud 1), NIGHT (setting) and the window shots.
