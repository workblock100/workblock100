## Part 1: the kit
1. Within 20 minutes: `GUIDE_WX20.md` from the contract, then batch 1: `L20.pass` (all four light kinds, `ambient`, `tint`, `bloom`, `lift`, `vignette`, flicker) and `SN20.snow`, `depthAt`, `wind`. After each batch run `node --check ep20_fx.js`, your harness, and look at a contact sheet of the batch on a dark and on a pale background.
2. `L20.pass` is the showpiece (the BLACKOUT scene lives or dies by it, and the DAYROOM and NIGHT scenes use it too). Implementation guidance: draw the lit picture first (that is the caller's job), then in the pass build the darkness in an offscreen canvas the size of the frame (reuse one canvas per size): fill it with `tint` at alpha `1 - ambient`, cut each light's reach out of it with `destination-out` using a cached radial or cone gradient sprite scaled by `power`, then draw the darkness over the picture; then add the lights' colour with `lighter` (a warm or cool tinted glow inside each light, stronger near the source) so a flashlight lights faces in its own colour and does not merely remove the dark; then `bloom` as a second soft additive pass on the cores. `lift` keeps a floor so a face in the dark is a dark blue face, never a black hole. Positions come through `ctx.getTransform()`. Everything is a pure function of its arguments (flicker from `FX18.rnd`). Test it with a character from `ep18_station.js` in a dark room lit by one cone, two points and a rect; look at it at full size once.
3. Snow quality bar: three depth layers (far: tiny, slow, dim, many; mid; near: big, soft, fast, out of focus), flakes with a slight sway that is not a sine wave to the eye (mix two frequencies per flake), wind that leans the whole field and gusts that visibly push it, no visible tiling or repetition across a 5-second shot, and no popping when a flake wraps. Frost must look like frost (branching ferns that grow, not a noise texture), sills and banks must look soft (blue shadow side, sparkle points). Breath must read as a cold puff, not steam.
4. Determinism and cost as in the contract (cached sprites, pure functions). The test: same call twice identical; frame t drawn first or last identical.
