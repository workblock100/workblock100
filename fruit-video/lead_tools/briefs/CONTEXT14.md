## THE ALIGNMENT CONTRACT (the reason the last two episodes were rebuilt: the user said "a lot of shit is off")
Our old audit never looked at text. The text gate (`txaudit.js`) does, and runs inside every audit of this episode (`textGate: true`). Your scenes must pass it AND look right to a human at full resolution.
1. **All text goes through the UI kit** (`ui.js`, `window.UI`; read `GUIDE_UI.md`): `UI.label`, `UI.sign`, `UI.pill`, `UI.panel`, `UI.page`/`UI.onRule`, `UI.table`, `UI.stack`, `UI.stamp`, `UI.tag`, `UI.ring`, `UI.fit`, `UI.rowX`. No raw `ctx.fillText`. No text drawn into offscreen/cached canvases (draw text live every frame; blit cached art and draw the text over it, naming the board with `UI.container`).
2. **Signs, posters, banners, boards and sheets are fully in the frame (hero text keeps 40 px from the edge) or entirely out of it. Never sliced.** If a camera move would slice one, move the sign, change the move, or hide it for those shots. A whip pan may carry text through the edge for a few frames (a fast pass of 300 px/s or more is exempt). Set dressing that only drifts through the edge during a pan is allowed to be cut ONLY with `hero: false, crop: true`; keep it away from the edges in every held shot. Nothing important in the caption zone (y 1380 to 1700) or the clock pill (x 48 to 500, y 116 to 244) except in scenes that turn those off (`noScrim`, `noChyron`).
3. **Text is upright and unmirrored.** At most about 12 degrees of tilt (stamps, stickers; pass `tilt` for more, sparingly). Never draw text with a flipped or sheared transform; a flipped character (`flip`) must not carry text.
4. **No text over text.** Two different strings never overlap. Lay tables out in columns.
5. **Hero text** (anything the viewer must read: signs, inserts, HUD, name tags, stamps, tags) is at least 30 px on screen, never covered, never under a caption or the pill. Set dressing is `hero: false`.
6. **Characters**: every face fully inside the frame, and no more than a sliver of a body cut by an edge unless it is walking in or out (`transit: true`). NO blurred foreground characters and no over-the-shoulder blur: a character in a shot is sharp and complete, or out of the shot. Background figures are small and keep clear of faces and text. No character stands in front of a hero sign.
7. **Things in a row line up**: feet on one floor line, equal gaps (`UI.rowX`), centred things on one axis (measure with the gate or a pixel check, do not eyeball), symmetric layouts truly symmetric, layout numbers on an 8 px grid (`UI.snap`).
8. **Look at it.** The gate cannot judge taste. After every audit pass, render stills at full size (every 0.5 s through your scenes, plus every moment a text element first appears) and look at them with the Read tool (at least 540 px wide; crop with PIL for detail). `python3 /tmp/shift_tools/atlas.py 14 <scene> 0.25` tiles a full-resolution crop of EVERY string in a scene: read every sheet, letter by letter. Your report must say what you looked at and what you changed because of it.

## Engine facts that bite (the rest are in GUIDE.md)
- Scene modules register on `window.__EP14_SCENES.<id>` inside an IIFE guarded by `if (!window.TIMELINE_EP14) return;`. Private code in other episodes is NOT exported: copy what you need with a comment saying where it came from. Never edit another episode's file or any file you do not own.
- Frame order: world draw, grade and bloom, `over()`, captions (scrim from y 1390, speaker chip 1386 to 1448, caption words to about y 1660), clock pill (x 48 to 480, y 116 to 184) and place label (y 196 to 242). Keep faces, hands, props and text out of y 1380 to 1700.
- `zoomTo(cx, cy, z)` centres the scene point (cx, cy); `faceZoom(o, z, up)` frames a face. `S6.shell` and `S6.lit` parse hex colour strings only. `box3` with fog needs explicit hex side, top and front. A squash lifts the shoes (use `plant: true`). A hard cut inside a motion-blur window shows a double exposure (set `blur` to 1 on cut frames). Never append a `//` comment mid-line in an edit (use `/* */`). `text()` squishes when wider than `maxW`: use `UI.fit`.
- Props held in hands: `hold`, `EXT.prop.<name>`, `PROP_BOX`; accessories: `EXT.acc.<name>`. Use the prefix `fp14_` for everything new so nothing collides.
- Characters are drawn with `drawChar(opts)` (opts from a constructor merged with x, y (feet), s). In the dollhouse a standing adult is `S14.S` = 0.44.
- Performance: 4 cores shared by several builders. Run ONE heavy process at a time (audit, sheets, previews). The page is shared: a module that throws at load breaks everyone's renders, so guard `if (!window.S14)` style dependencies and syntax-check after every edit.
