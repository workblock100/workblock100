# Brief E14-A: scenes `title` and `end` (Episode 14): the two design scenes

Files you own: `ep14_title.js` (scenes `title` and `end`, registering `window.__EP14_SCENES.title` and `.end`). Read `/tmp/shift_tools/briefs/CONTEXT14.md` first (the episode, the documentary look, the alignment contract), then `ep13_title.js` (a finished title and end card under the text gate: how `UI.stack`, `UI.rowX`, `UI.sign` and the sound-cue timing were used), `ep12_title.js` and `ep11_title.js` (the series' title style: SHIFT HAPPENS in yellow Bangers with a dark outline, an EPISODE pill, the title in big condensed type, a "THANKS FOR WATCHING" end card with the cast and a teaser pill). Episode graphics palette and type (CONTEXT14): field-guide cream `#f2e8cf`, forest green `#24402e`, ochre `#c98b2c`, ink `#1d1a16`, stamp red `#c23a2b`, series yellow `#ffe135`. Fonts: Bangers (stamps, big names), Nunito 900 (labels), Nunito italic for Latin names, Lucky sparingly.

## Scene `title` (5.2 s; set `noChyron: true, noScrim: true`)
Beat `logo` 0.2 to 5.0. Sound cues (scene-local seconds; `python3 /tmp/shift_tools/cues.py 14 title`): `birds` at 0.2, `theme7` at 0.2 (the series sting: its landing is at about 2.6), `textding` at 2.7 (the lettering lands). Land the sun, the swell and the title on those.
Concept: the opening of a **nature documentary**. A silent blue pre-dawn; the sun comes up behind a ridge; the sky goes from indigo to rose to gold; god rays and a long, warm lens flare sweep across; a flock of birds crosses the frame; in the foreground, the Fruit Bowl building stands on its hill as a **dollhouse silhouette: every room's window a small glowing rectangle** (use `S14.rect/names` for the geometry if it helps, but draw your own silhouette: the title is a graphic, not a scene of the building; do not call `S14.back`, which is for the interior scenes). A slow crane-up/push-in over 5 s. The title then lands: "SHIFT HAPPENS" (series style), an "EPISODE 14" pill, "THE SURVEY" (huge, condensed, a gold shine sweeping across it once) and a small italic tagline "A natural history of the State." All through `UI.stack`, perfectly centred, nothing within 40 px of the edge, nothing under the sun's brightest spot that would hurt legibility (check contrast). New idea required: do not repeat the vault dial (Ep13), the projector (Ep12) or the time card (Ep11).
Easter eggs welcome if they never touch text: a star-shaped cloud, a tiny sedan on the road at the bottom, a pigeon with a clipboard.

## Scene `end` (8.0 s; `noChyron: true, noScrim: true`; label 'THANKS FOR WATCHING')
Beat `card` 0.1 to 7.6 (`theme7` at 0.1). The end card of the series with a documentary twist: **a specimen wall**, a museum gallery wall (warm plaster, a picture rail, a hard overhead spot) hung with six glass-front **shadow boxes** in a 3 x 2 grid (equal boxes, equal gaps via `UI.rowX`/`UI.gridX`): Strawberry, Lemon, Mango, Clementine, Mr. Stateman (`A14.stateman`) and the Kumquat (`A14.kumquat`); the Moon framed as a round window in the top right of the stack of text, as on earlier end cards if it fits. Each character is "pinned" in their box (they are alive: a blink, a breath, a tiny fidget; Stateman raises one eyebrow; the Kumquat checks his clipboard), with a brass plate under the box (`UI.sign`, two lines: the Latin name in italic and the role in capitals; the species labels are in CONTEXT14 so they match the rest of the film). Above the wall a centred text stack (`UI.stack`): "THANKS FOR WATCHING", "SHIFT HAPPENS" in series style, "THE SURVEY". Below the wall a yellow gag line: "BREAKS TAKEN TODAY: 6 (MANDATORY)" (the counter ticks up from 0 to 6 one by one in the first 3 seconds: the six staff sat down; it must never move the text: draw the digits in a fixed-width slot) and the teaser pill "NEXT: EPISODE 15 / THE MERGER" (a `UI.pill`, same design as the Episode 13 teaser). Camera flashes or dust in the light every second are welcome if they never cross text. No cut figures, no overlaps, everything on the centre axis x = 540.
