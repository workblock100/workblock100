A globe artichoke: a green dome of overlapping scale-leaves (eleven visible rings of petals, each ring a little darker or lighter, tips slightly purple), a short stem, a face in the upper middle (smooth, calm, heavy-lidded confident eyes, a slight smile, a tiny gold CEO pin).
