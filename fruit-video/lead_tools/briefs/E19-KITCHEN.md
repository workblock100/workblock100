## Chef Cabbage (new)
Dietary manager: a green cabbage head with layered leaves, a tall white toque, a stained apron with a name tag CHEF CABBAGE, a ladle, a mustache of leaf veins, a tired and dignified face. Deadpan, enormous patience. Built like the other cast (draw with `drawChar`, `yaw` turnarounds, expressions, a held ladle and tray). Voice: slow, low, British; his lines are long and flat.
