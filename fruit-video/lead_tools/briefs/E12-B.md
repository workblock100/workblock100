## The set (`ep12_room.js`)
A conference room, perspective, on the S6 shell (copy the shell call from `ep10_set.js`; hex colours only). Warm fluorescent ceiling (two rows of panels, one flickering), a long folding table with a white cloth and eight chairs down its sides, a whiteboard on the back wall (text: IN-SERVICE: DIGNITY FIRST, and a list with one item crossed out), a projector screen (roll-down, white, a little sagging at the corners) on the far wall, a projector cart with the projector on it and its fan, a wall clock (the scene clock pill is separate), a corkboard with flyers (LOST CAT, WHO TOOK MY YOGURT, Luigi's pizza), a stack of pizza boxes on a rolling cart near the door (the pizza that arrives in `signin`), a water-cooler, a potted plant. Export `window.S12room = { shell(...), table(...), screen(...), ... }` with the layout numbers at the top. The screen content is drawn by the slides scene (pass a `slide` drawing function in), so keep the screen as a rectangle with a `drawSlide(ctx, x, y, w, h, t)` hook.
