- `drillLog(ctx, o)`: a clipboard with a printed form: FIRE DRILL LOG; rows DATE (MONDAY), TIME (7:00 PM), FIRE (NONE), EVACUATION TIME (0 MIN), SIGNED (a scrawl), `o.stamp` 0..1 for a red PASS stamp. Legible at insert size (the end card uses it too).
- `smokeDetector(ctx, o)`: a white ceiling-mounted detector with a vent pattern and a small LED (`o.led` 0..1 blinks; draw a glow with `FX18.glow` when it exists), a TEST button.
