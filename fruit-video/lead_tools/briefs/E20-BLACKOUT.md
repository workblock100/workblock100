## Part 2: the scene `blackout` (6:12 PM, scene-local seconds). The technical showpiece of the episode: LIGHT

Everything else is deep blue, never black (L2): use `L20.pass` with `ambient` about .2 to .3 and `lift`, so faces are readable dark-blue shapes with rim light, and the lit faces glow. Photosensitivity: ONE dip (see CONTEXT20).
