## The score (`build_music15.py`): "corporate positive, turning sour"
Write `score(script, scenes, lines, N)` returning a (2, N) stereo bed. Palette: a bright ukulele or pizzicato strum, claps and finger snaps, marimba and glockenspiel, whistling (a synthesized whistle), a friendly synth pad, upbeat drums, then a minor-mode twist: the same tune slowed, detuned, with a drone and a muted trumpet. **Motifs (write them, use them like leitmotifs):** the **Merge theme** (title: a cheerful eight-bar corporate jingle that ends in a satisfying two-chord resolution on the merge (2.30 s) and comes back at the end card), the **Binder jingle** (four cheerful notes with a rising scale that plays under Binder's lines and curdles into a minor version on the grin), the **Artichoke theme** (smooth elevator-jazz sax over a soft Rhodes, always a bit too smooth), **Onion's clarinet sob** (a descending whimper), **Tomato's pizzicato** (perky, indecisive: alternates major and minor). Per scene: title: the Merge theme (0 to 2.3 sets up, resolves on `merge`, shimmer at 4.0); announce: stiff, upbeat office stock music under Melon; the Artichoke theme over the TV (the video call's band-limited); a hush on "You're frozen" and the awkward `stuck` silence (no music: a room tone and a distant cough); the Binder jingle for the assistant; a drumroll build into the dialog; a sweeping hit on the merge; dock: heavy: low brass, timpani and a taiko for the crane, tension strings during the lowering (a rising riser that peaks at `dockimpact` 5.82 and then a total drop-out of the score for the impact tail), then a tuba-and-pizzicato "salad vs pizza" comic march, the zipper-merge as a polite string-quartet minuet that stumbles into a bonk, Onion's sob under the end; model1: the Executive Suite as a pompous brass fanfare, the sinking as a downward glissando (a trombone slide, then a low thump), Remove Duplicates as a quick xylophone run and five tiny poofs, the synergy line a gleeful fanfare cut off; model2: the fill as a rising arpeggio ripple that crowds into a wall of sound at `crowd`, then a cut to near-silence for "I'm one person." and "I'm tired." (deadpan); the lookup error a descending two-note; replace: a manic typing rhythm and the flips as a rising percussion run, the counter as a snare roll climbing, then the "Pending!" answers as a call-and-response with a stab each; close: the banner as a big hopeful orchestral swell that is slightly too much, the Artichoke theme with a tiny celeste heartbeat, the undo as a cut-off, the last offer in a minor-key Binder jingle with a slow drone, and the `pending` stinger and `sting` at the end; end: the Merge theme reprise (cheerful again, but one note flat at the end).
Quality bar: this episode is meant to be a leap in polish. Real arrangements (bass, drums or pulse, harmony, a lead), dynamics, stereo width, scene music that ends where the visuals end. Keep the dock's impact the loudest moment without clipping.
