## What you build

### 1. The kit `S15`: the tower
`S15` extends `S14` (call S14, do not copy it). It draws **the Fruit Bowl dollhouse with the Greenleaf dollhouse stacked on top of it**, plus the crane, the ground, an Executive Suite floor and the signs. All other builders (E15-C: model1/model2, E15-B: replace, E15-A: close) use your kit, so the API must be clean and documented in `GUIDE_S15.md` within your first 90 minutes (a first version drawing the stacked tower with the parameters below, even with rough Greenleaf rooms), then improved.
- **Greenleaf Senior Living** (4 rooms wide, 3 floors, the same room size as S14 so the stack aligns; GREEN, white and bamboo; plants, a vertical garden wall, wicker, wooden slats, sunlight; original and warm, NOT a copy of Fruit Bowl with a recolour): top floor: Sky Garden, Salad Bar, Meditation Room, Sunroom; middle: Wellness Room, Nurses' Station (smaller than Fruit Bowl's, with an "ORGANIC" sign), Med Room, Art Room; bottom floor (the one that lands on Fruit Bowl's roof): Lobby with a vertical garden, Dining Room, Therapy Garden, Fish Tank Lounge. Each room has its place label plate like S14. A roof sign: GREENLEAF SENIOR LIVING (leaf logo, original). Reuse S14's room shell drawing and item library (`S14.drawItem`) as much as you can, with a green palette, and add new items (`fp15_` prefix): plants, hanging pots, a salad bar with sneeze guard, a fish tank with fish, bamboo screens, a yoga mat, a kombucha tap, a living wall.
- **Stacking**: `S15.tower(lt, st)` where `st` has: `stack` (0 = the Greenleaf block hangs in the air above Fruit Bowl, 1 = docked: you will drive it from the physics), `blockPose` (x, y, angle, from the sim), `squat` (the Fruit Bowl block compresses vertically by up to about 40 px with a spring), `sink` (0..1+: the whole tower sinks by `sink` x one floor height into the ground), `exec` (0..1: the Executive Suite floor is placed on top: marble, gold trim, a panoramic window, a desk the size of a car, a putting green, a pizza oven and a salad bar side by side, a CEO sign), `signs` (an object of sign texts so a scene can change them: `S15.sign(id)` rect and text hooks for `SH15.flipText`: ROOF, BREAK ROOM, the staff board, the door plates), `rooms` (door open/closed, lights, props) like `S14`.
- **The ground and the lot**: a cutaway of the ground under the building (soil in layers, roots, a worm, a buried pipe) so that when the tower sinks the lobby goes underground and stays visible (its door, its sign LOBBY, the reception desk in the dark, a lamp), and a ladder and an open window at the new street level (a way in "through the window").
- **The crane**: a tall tower crane (lattice mast, jib, counter-jib with weights, cab with a tiny Binder in a hard hat (use `A15.binderSmall` and `fp15_hardhat` when they exist, a placeholder until then), a trolley, a hook block) beside the building; cables are PHYSICS ropes (from `PHYS2D`: a rope per cable, the hook block and the load as bodies); the Greenleaf block hangs from four cables; the same crane later places the Executive Suite (E15-C drives it with `S15.crane(lt, st)`: expose `S15.craneRig(...)` so a scene can ask for any kinematic hook path and receive rope points).
- **API sugar**: `S15.rect(id)` for any room of either building in tower coordinates (and the world position of the floors given `sink`/`squat`), `S15.floorY(i)`, `S15.walk/run` (inherit S14's), `S15.stairs` (a staircase/chute connecting the two buildings at the join: a hatch in Fruit Bowl's ceiling, a spiral stair and a green salad chute (a slide)), `S15.cam(view)` helpers: wide of the whole tower, a floor, a room, a join.
- Performance: a full-tower frame must draw in under about 0.4 s (cache the static building art into offscreen canvases at 2x for zoom, never cache text: draw labels live through `UI`).
