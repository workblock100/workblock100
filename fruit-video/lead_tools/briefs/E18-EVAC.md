## The scene (scene-local seconds)
The snooze has run out (8:10 PM, ten minutes after the alarm scene): the horn is back, muffled by the shut doors (the sound handles it); the strobes are back in the corridor and flash through the day room's door glass and window. The `horn3` cue is at about 0.5 s (`pachime` 0.2); use it as `k = 0` for `FX18.strobe(k)` / `FX18.hornOn(k)` exactly as ALARM does. A small toast on the wall tablet: SNOOZE ENDED (UI kit).
- `open` 0.2-1.5: the DAY ROOM in warm darkness lit by the TV: the Yule log (`FX18.flame` kind 'log' on the TV screen, plus its warm glow `FX18.glow` / `FX18.cone` spilling on the room); the residents in a semicircle of chairs seen from behind (Banana, Pear, Cranberry named; others generic), blankets, a cup of tea; the red strobe pulse through the window against the warm light (two light sources fighting); `yulecrackle` 0.3.

(she waves her clipboard); `r1` 3.93 the residents, all at once, not turning: "We are watching the fire." (the chorus; turn only their heads, a quarter turn, deadpan); `l2` 5.69 Lemon "That is a video of a fire."; `c1` 7.46 Mr. Banana, lit by the flame, "It is the best part of the day." - `line` 12.01-15.01 (3.0 s): they ALL get up and leave in an orderly line, wheelchairs, walkers, a slow shuffle, to the fire door at the room's far end (a tracking shot along the line, and then the TOP-DOWN view): `wheelchair` 12.01, `cartrattle` 12.61. (the counter chip reads 38, 39, 40 on her three words; hit the words from `LN.m1.words`). `l4` 18.33 Lemon "Forty. Good." (a tick on the clipboard).
- `ch1` 19.63 the forty Strawberries pour out of the hallway behind her (the Episode 15 crowd, now flooding through the same fire door; the sim handles them as a second wave that jams the door): "I'm one person." - `end` 26.01 sting 26.11: the final top-down shot: 80 heads at the assembly point, the strobe still pulsing.

## The sim (`ep18_evac_sim.js`)
About 80 agents in two waves: 40 residents (mixed: walkers at 0.6 m/s, wheelchairs at 0.9, a few slow shufflers) and 40 Strawberries (1.4 m/s, a med cup each) released at `ch1` (about 19.6 s). A simple social-force or velocity-obstacle crowd model with a door of one-person width (the fire door), queueing and the arch-shaped crowd that forms in front of a door, a corridor with walls, deterministic (seeded, fixed time step, no `Math.random`), sampled at any scene-local time. The sim reports for the scene: positions and headings at time t, and the number that have passed the door line at time t (the counter reads from this; make the count hit exactly 38, 39, 40 at Mango's words and 80 at Lemon's "Eighty."; you may scale the time-lapse so it does). Keep the sim a pure `build()` module so the lead can also run it from node.
