## The scene (41.0 s, clock 4:48 PM, label THE EXIT CONFERENCE; `python3 /tmp/shift_tools/cues.py 14 verdict` prints every cue in scene-local seconds)
The longest and funniest scene. A full-frame **conference room** of your own: long table, the staff seated along one side (Melon at the head), Stateman and the Kumquat standing at the other end with the clipboards, a tall window, venetian blind light, a wall screen. Around the table are stacks of binders (the same binders as `arrive`). One big prop owns the room: the **severity board**, a Plinko board standing on an easel at the head of the table (a 12-slot Plinko: pegs in a staggered field, a ball, the slots labelled A to L, grouped by colour in four bands of three the way the real scope and severity grid is: A B C green, D E F yellow, G H I orange, J K L red; a bell on top). Beyond that, a Statement of Deficiencies form on a clipboard-sized panel in the Kumquat's hands (`UI.table`: TAG, FINDING, SEVERITY) that fills in as the findings land.
