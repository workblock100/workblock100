- **AUDIT LESSONS FROM EPISODES 18 AND 19 (each cost a fix; avoid them up front)**: (1) the audit steps on the renderer's exact frame times, so a shot boundary frame is the NEW shot: look up shots with a small epsilon (`lt >= sh.s - 1e-6`) and snap shot starts to frames (`snapT`); (2) never draw a character or prop at scale 0 (the first frame of an `eb()` pop): skip it if its scale is under .02; (3) pointers and cursors must never cross hero text; (4) a character entering or leaving the frame is either wholly inside or wholly outside by the time it is drawn at a frame edge: no half faces at the edge, no face box 2 px past it; (5) bodies and props never cover a face box (keep the cast spread; use `inFrame` checks); (6) do not draw the same text twice at the same place; (7) letter-flip animations only as dressing text; (8) a snowdrift, a scarf tail, a hat or a flake never covers a face box.
- **LEGIBILITY (new, hard; Episode 19's weaknesses were small dim wides, a beat smeared by whip pans and steam that vanished on pale walls)**:
  L1 A character who is speaking is at least 300 px tall in the frame while speaking, with the face lit and unobstructed. A wide may be held at most 2.0 s and must still show every speaker at 220 px or more; establishing wides of a building or a lot (no speakers) are exempt.
  L2 Brightness. Outside the dark beats listed below, no frame has a mean luminance under 0.20 (0 to 1, on the final picture) or more than 45 percent of its pixels under 0.08. Night is deep BLUE, never black; every speaking face is lit by something that is in the frame (a screen, a lamp, a window, a flashlight). The dark beats: `blackout` from 0.3 to 12.9 s (the cascade, the lines in the dark and the count, until the generator's ramp begins; the stopwatch reads 10.0 at 12.4 s). Even there, every face that speaks is lit by something in the frame. Check with `python3 /tmp/shift_tools/luma_scene.py 20 <scene> 0.5` (prints a table and flags frames; exit 1 if any frame outside the dark beats is flagged).
  L3 Camera speed: no move faster than 700 px/s except a deliberate whip between two shots right at a shot boundary (at most 6 frames), and no whip inside a gag. A speaking face never smears.
  L4 Weather readability: snow reads on dark AND on pale backgrounds (`tone` option, a faint cool rim on pale walls); breath puffs get a soft darker edge; nothing white on white; steam and breath are thicker than you think.
- PHOTOSENSITIVITY (hard rule, in force from Episode 18): never more than 3 flashes in any one second; the blackout is ONE dip: the lights go out in a cascade over about 1.2 s (at most two quick flickers before each room dies), and come back as a ramp of at least 0.4 s with at most one warm-up flicker; after the cascade there is at most ONE full-frame opposing luminance change per second. No strobing candles (flicker amplitude small and slow), no flashing screens. The flash check on the final render must say safe.
- **The timeline is frozen**: never change a line, a pause or a cue offset (the lead owns `gen_script20.py`, `script_ep20.json`, `ep20.js`, `build/ep20/*`). Tie actions to beat starts, spoken words (`lines[].words` in `build/ep20/timeline.json`; `LN[id].words[i][1|2]` are line-relative seconds) and the sound cues.
