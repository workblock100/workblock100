# Brief E12-A: scenes `title` and `end` (Episode 12)

File you own: `ep12_title.js`, registering `window.__EP12_SCENES.title` and `window.__EP12_SCENES.end`. Copy the pattern of `ep10_title.js` (title and end card). Read `ep11_title.js` too (the punch clock title) so the family of the series' title treatment holds, but make this one a different idea.

## title (5.2 s, `noChyron: true`, `noScrim: true`, no clock, no label)
Beat `logo` (0.20 to 5.00). Cues: theme7 at 0.00; textding at 0.12; fanwhir at 0.00 (a projector fan spinning up, long); clack at 0.45, 1.10, 1.75 and 2.40 (a projector changing slides).
Concept: a projector in a dark conference room. A warm beam cuts diagonally from the upper left across the frame with dust in it. The projected slide fills the frame: slide 1 DIGNITY FIRST (in a clean slide font), slide 2 a red rubber stamp MANDATORY (lands on the clack at 1.10), slide 3 SHIFT HAPPENS (the series logo, Bangers, yellow with a dark outline, as in the other titles), slide 4 EPISODE 12 and THE IN-SERVICE large at the end. Hold the finished logo from about 3.0 s to the end (5.0 s) with a slow push-in. Keep the logo block fully legible and nothing moving over it after 3.0 s.
The projector's light should flicker once on the first clack (a bulb hiccup), and there is a little fan-blade shadow across the screen while it spins.

## end (8.0 s, `noChyron: true`, `noScrim: true`, label THANKS FOR WATCHING via the script)
Beat `card` (0.10 to 7.60). Cue: theme7 at 0.00.
Concept: the night sky and skyline of the earlier end cards (see `ep11_title.js` `end` for the family), the Moon in the sky (A7.moon), the cast in a row: Strawberry (A11.straw) holding a framed certificate, Clementine, Lemon, Mango, the Administrator in front. The certificate reads CERTIFIED IN SAYING NO (legible, large). Text in this order: the title lockup SHIFT HAPPENS / THE IN-SERVICE (0.1), THANKS FOR WATCHING (0.5), "For every nurse who was told it was voluntary." (about 1.0), "There is no slide for no." in yellow (about 2.8), "You are worth every R." (about 4.2). Then a teaser pill at the bottom: NEXT: EPISODE 13 / THE FLOAT POOL (about 5.4), in the same pill style as Episode 11's end card (`ep11_title.js`).
Nothing on the frame edges. Keep the bottom third clear for the teaser pill; keep the cast above y 1500.
