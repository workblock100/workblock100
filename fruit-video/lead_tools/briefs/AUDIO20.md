## The upgrades to hear
1. **Outdoor and indoor acoustics**: `lot` is outside in falling snow: almost no reverb, everything absorbed and close, a hush with wind (a low rumble with a gusting tonal howl; gusts at the cue times `plowscrape`, `plowdump`, and as a slow independent swell), boots creaking and crunching in snow (use the footsteps from `build/ep20/events.json` when they exist: in `lot` map every footstep to a snow crunch; indoors normal floor steps), breath. The glass-door gust (`doorgust`) opens the wind for a moment (the same trick as Episode 18's door-keyed low-pass, here as a brief wideband rush).
2. **The ten seconds** (`blackout`): `powerdown` is a whole building losing power: the fluorescent ballast buzz cutting out, fans and compressors spinning down in pitch (a falling whine over 1.5 s), the HVAC sigh, the exit-sign relays clunking (`relay` x3, panned across the building left to right), then a thick hush with only wind, breaths, the stopwatch ticks (`stopwatchticks`: a quiet digital tick per second, 10 ticks across `count` with a subtle accelerating tension pad that is NOT music), flashlight clicks and Binder's `lowbattery` (a descending two-tone device chime and a power-down whine). `genstart` (12.33): a diesel starter cranking (rising), two coughs, the catch, a steady deep rumble that fills the building (low-passed through the walls) with a transfer-switch clunk at `relay` 13.13; from then on the room tone is the emergency lights' buzz and the generator's distant drone instead of the normal hum.
3. **The time-lapse** (`night` `lapse`, 10.65 to 18.65 s): read `build/ep20/lapse.json` (written by the NIGHT builder: ABSOLUTE seconds, 10 Hz, `{t, worldMin, speed, sun, moonX}`; if it does not exist yet use a fixed curve: ease in 0.7 s, 8x hold, ease out 0.9 s, and say so in your report). At 7:00 the snow's silence arrives (the wind drops away to a hush, a faint distant plow, a single tiny glint tone for the sunrise).
4. **Voice-through-device** realism (speakerphone, phone, TV, cab, buried, behind the weather): see above; they are the sound of the episode's jokes.
