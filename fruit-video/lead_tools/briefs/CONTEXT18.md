## What is NEW in Episode 18 (the upgrades; the viewer should SEE and HEAR each one; do your part of them well)
1. **FX18**: the first effects kit of the series: flames, foam, smoke, sparks, strobes, light cones, washes (FX18_API.md).
2. **Emergency lighting as a lighting pass**: the strobes pulse with the horn in the real temporal-three pattern (`FX18.hornOn`, `FX18.strobe`), a red emergency tint, glowing exit signs, flashlight cones. Dark scenes with a coloured light source, not flat daylight.
3. **The corridor of doors** (ALARM): a long one-point-perspective corridor with six magnetic fire doors that close one after another from far to near, a tracking camera, and a sound that follows: the horn is muffled a step more with every door (the sound builder reads `build/ep18/door_events.json`).
4. **Crowd simulation** (EVAC): about 80 agents flowing to and queueing at a door (DOM-free deterministic sim, `ep18_evac_sim.js`), plus a TOP-DOWN floor plan view, a camera angle the series has never used.
5. **A physics prank with thrust** (MARSHAL): the extinguisher as a rigid body with a jet; foam leaves the moving nozzle (`FX18.foamJet` with an emitter that is a function of time).
6. **Sound tied to picture state**: the alarm's muffling follows the doors; the PA voice is processed; the silence after the snooze is designed.
7. **New props**: fire door, alarm panel, pull station, horn/strobe, exit sign, extinguisher, smoke detector, evacuation map, drill log (`window.P18`).
