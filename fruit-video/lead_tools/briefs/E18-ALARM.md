## Part 1: the props (first 30 minutes; the MARSHAL, EVAC, STATION and FX builders need them)
`window.P18 = window.P18 || {}` in `ep18_props.js`, signatures documented in a comment block at the top, in this order: `fireDoor(ctx, o)` (a steel-grey leaf in a frame with a wire-glass window, a KEEP CLOSED sign on it, a magnetic holder on the wall with an LED, `o.close` 0..1 (0 open against the wall, 1 shut), `o.prop` null|'cart'|'chair'|'wedge' holding it open, `o.glow` for the LED; frontal elevation; the corridor's perspective version is private to your scene), `exitSign(ctx, o)` (glowing green EXIT with a running figure, `o.glow`, `o.flicker`; the title card's variant uses a running strawberry: support `o.runner:'strawberry'`), `pullStation(ctx, o)` (red, white FIRE lettering, a lever PULL DOWN, `o.pulled` 0..1), `hornStrobe(ctx, o)` (a wall or ceiling horn with a strobe lens, `o.level` 0..1 from `FX18.strobe`, uses `FX18.lamp` when it exists), `panel(ctx, o)` (the fire alarm control panel: grey metal box with a green/amber/red LED column, a two-line LCD, buttons ACK, SILENCE, RESET, DRILL; `o.state`: 'normal' | 'drill' | 'alarm' | 'snoozed' | 'trouble'; `o.lcd` = two strings; `o.leds`). Text through the UI kit. Make them look like the real things (a nurse will recognise them): the panel with `SYSTEM NORMAL`, the exit sign's colours, the pull station's textured red.

The fire alarm panel and the pull station are on the near left wall, a wall tablet (Binder) beside the panel. Strawberry has her med cart in the corridor (the med pass never stops), Mango a linen cart, Lemon a clipboard, Onion with her clipboard and a tissue, the Marshal at the panel. ## Part 3: the door events for the sound (REQUIRED)
`ep18_alarm_sim.js` (DOM-free) holds the door timeline: each door's close start, slam time and rebound, and the cart bonk. `tools_door_events.js` (node, no DOM) writes `build/ep18/door_events.json`: an array of `{ "t": <ABSOLUTE seconds = scene start 77.479 + scene-local slam time>, "name": "doorslam" | "doorbonk" | "doorlatch", "door": 0..5, "depth": 0..1 (0 = at the camera, 1 = far end), "pan": -1..1, "gain": 0..1 }`, sorted by `t`, and also the horn state `{ "t":..., "name": "hornstate", "closed": <number of doors shut so far> }` after every slam. The picture reads the SAME table, so the slam you see is the slam that is heard (within one frame). Run it once your timing is final and again after any change; tell the lead.
