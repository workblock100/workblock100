# SHIFT HAPPENS, Episode 12: "The In-Service". Story and world

Read /tmp/shift_tools/GUIDE.md first (how to build and check a scene). This file is the world, the cast and the story.

## The world
"Fruit Bowl Rehab & Nursing": a nursing home staffed by fruit. One 3-to-11 PM shift per episode. A workplace comedy told as a deadpan soap opera. Nobody is evil; the system is the joke. The cast loves each other even when the building does not love them back.
Format: 1080x1920, voiced, captioned, scenes of 5 to 50 seconds. A visual gag on almost every beat. A reaction on every punchline.

## Cast (voice ids match the script's `who`)
- **Strawberry** (`straw`; use `A11.straw`, the per diem look: steth, bandage, PER DIEM badge, no staff badge). Our lead. Dry, tired, precise. Per diem since Episode 11 (per diem = agency-rate, on-call, "not in my contract"). Her arc in the series is learning to say no. In this episode she says "No." for the first time, twice.

## Running gags to keep (the series' continuity)
- **Per diem / "not in my contract" / "under protest".** Per diem staff attend in-service voluntarily.
- **The pizza budget.** Pizza is always funded; staff are not. The budget chart is 97 percent pizza.
- **The pen** given "to share". The Administrator's gifts.
- **The good cuff**, behind the Q-word sign on the second shelf of the supply closet. The Q-word itself is never said on screen (the sign reads QUIET with the Q crossed out).
- **Sure!** Clementine's answer to everything.
- **Lemon's reports.** Nobody reads them. (They turn out to be read.)
- **The R that is missing** (the cake, the banner: GOODBYE STRAWBER_Y, WELCOME BACK STRAWBERY).
- **The DIRECTOR OF NO nameplate** on the Administrator's desk; the Administrator's diploma CERTIFIED IN SAYING NO.
- **Money**: staff $34/hr, agency $118/hr, overtime $177/hr.

## Tone
Deadpan. Every gag is a small beat with a set-up and a payoff. Short lines. Nobody shouts except Mango, and the Administrator (who is cheerful about everything). The emotional turn (the first "No.") must land quietly: the room goes still, a balloon stops, a pen stops.
