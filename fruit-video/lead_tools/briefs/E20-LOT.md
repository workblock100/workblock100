## Part 2: the scene `lot` (scene-local seconds)
Setting: the parking lot of the nursing home at 4:30 PM in a blizzard at dusk: a blue-violet sky going dark, the long low building at the back with warm lit windows and a lit entrance canopy, a row of six cars along the lot (colours: red, teal, yellow, grey, white, plum; `snow` .35 at the start), lamp posts with haloes in the falling snow, plowed berms along the edges, the plow truck with an amber beacon (a slow sweep, not a strobe). The staff are in the foreground in winter gear: Strawberry (red beanie), Mango, Lemon, Tomato, Onion; their breath puffs (`SN20.breath`); snow dusts their hats and shoulders (`snow` option). Wind leans the snow and the scarves (`wind`). Colour: cool blues and violets outside, warm yellow from the windows and the canopy.
- `open` 0.20-1.20: a wide of the lot, building lit at the back, the cars, the snow, the plow's beacon sweeping from the left edge; the staff small in the foreground (this is an establishing wide, held at most 2 s; speakers are not in it yet).
- `plow` 1.30-3.30 (`plowengine` 0.1 s before the beat, `plowscrape` 1.50, `plowdump` 2.70): the plow truck crosses the lot (headlights in the snow: `SN20.beam`), the blade pushing a wave of snow ALONG the row of cars: each car's `snow` goes from .35 to .9 as the berm reaches it (do it car by car, in a staggered wave: one car per 0.25 s), `SN20.spray` off the blade, the exhaust (`FX18.smoke` at `exhaust`); at `plowdump` the last car goes under with a `SN20.flurry`. A tracking camera that follows the blade, then settles on the truck. The cars end as smooth hills.
- `tu1` 3.35 close on Turnip in the cab window (A20.turnip, `cab`): "I plowed the lot." (deadpan, the mug; the wipers) | `m1` 5.07 Mango (winter gear, snow on her hat, breath): "You plowed in the cars." (a flat look at the hills behind her) | `tu2` 6.54 Turnip again, identical, and he raises the mug, drives off at 7.14 (`plowengine`), exhaust, the beacon sweeping the lot as he leaves.
- `leave` 8.30-9.20: the truck leaves, the wind, the staff look at the hills: an over-the-shoulder shot.
- `s1` 9.25 Strawberry "Where is my car?" (close: breath, snow on her hat) | `l1` 10.21 Lemon points "That one." (a mound among identical mounds) | `s2` 11.08 "That is a hill." (flat; the mound is a perfect smooth hill with a mirror and the antenna sticking out; it must read as HER car: a red mirror).
- `dig` 12.11-14.51 (`shovel` cues at 12.41, 13.01, 13.61; `snowdump` 14.11): Strawberry digs with a shovel (A20.shovelThrow with SN20.spray); the hole fills with falling and drifting snow almost as fast as she digs it (a short loop of dig, throw, refill, in a tracking mid-shot); at 14.11 she opens the car door and a block of snow slides out and buries her to the knees. Keep her face visible.
- `t1` 14.56 Tomato "Snowman contest!" (A20.snowman growing from build .2 to 1 during the next seconds, a party hat; a party whistle at 14.71; Tomato bounces) | `o1` 15.81 Onion "It is a fall risk." (she steps onto a patch of ice; the slip at 16.71, the thud at 17.11: she slides on her back across the lot, a spray of snow, the snowman's head tumbles after her; use `phys2d.js` if you want the head to roll) | `o2` 17.38 Onion "Incident report." (flat on her back in the snow, holding a clipboard straight up and writing, crying, the tears freezing into tiny ice beads: `A15.cryOn`).
- `ring` 19.03-19.93 (`phonering` 19.03): Strawberry's phone rings in her mitten; she answers on speaker. A UI-kit picture-in-picture appears (rounded video-call frame, top right, clear of the faces): `A20.ditchPicture({ eat: 0 })`: Eggplant in her small car nose-down in a snowy ditch, hazard lights blinking slowly, NO pizza visible yet (the end card reveals it). `e1` 19.98 "I am stuck." | `s3` 21.04 "Where?" | `e2` 21.58 "A ditch." (she points out of the windshield at the ditch) | `m2` 22.45 "Which ditch?" | `e3` 23.36 "A ditch." (the same gesture) | `s4` 24.29 "When will you be here?" | `e4` 25.36 "Spring." (she settles back, pulls her scarf up, calm) . Each line has the speaker or the picture-in-picture as the shot; the listeners react in turn; no frame is static.
- `end` 26.47-27.67 (sting 26.57): all five stand in a row in the snow looking at the lit building, small and cold, breath rising: hold.
Shots at least every 3 s (the plow beat is one tracking shot of about 2 s); every line has a reaction; all speakers 300 px or taller while speaking (L1).
