# WX20_API: the weather and light kit contract for Episode 20 (`ep20_fx.js`: `window.SN20` snow and cold, `window.L20` light). Written by the lead; implemented by the WX builder; used by every scene builder

Everybody codes against THIS file at once. Names, argument shapes and units are FIXED. The WX builder may ADD functions and options (document them at the top of `ep20_fx.js` and in `GUIDE_WX20.md`), never rename or reshape these. Until a function lands, a scene draws its own stand-in and swaps in the real one when `typeof SN20.name === 'function'` (check at DRAW time). Read `FX18_API.md` first: the conventions below are the same.

## Conventions (all functions)
- They draw into the current `ctx`, in WORLD units (the same transform the characters use, after the scene's camera), unless marked SCREEN. Pure functions of their arguments and `t`: no `Math.random`, no `Date`, no state that depends on how many frames were drawn before. Randomness only from `FX18.rnd(seed, k)` (`ep18_fx.js` loads first). Frame N must be identical in every worker and every run (4 parallel workers, segmented renders).
- Each function saves and restores `ctx` state. Cost: about 2 ms per call at most (snow with 3 layers at density 1: 1.5 ms); the light pass at most 8 ms. Pre-render soft sprites (flakes, crystals, drifts) once into offscreen canvases (`FX18.sprite`) and `drawImage` them.
- `t` = scene-local seconds (the `lt` the scene receives). Snow depth, frost and sill heights are given by the caller as numbers (the scene computes them from `lt` and the episode's clock, see `SN20.depthAt`).
- NEVER cover hero text, a face or the caption zone (lowest 15 percent of the frame) with more than a light, small, low-alpha haze. Snow is in-world: behind or beside characters; the near (out-of-focus) layer is only for shots where it does not cross a face (`fore: false` leaves it out).
- Readability (LEGIBILITY L4 of CONTEXT20): `tone: 'light'` (default) draws flakes white with a faint cool-blue rim and a tiny dark core shadow so they read on pale walls; `tone: 'dark'` draws plain white flakes with a soft glow for night skies and dark rooms.

## The clock of the snow (shared by every scene so the same window gets deeper over the episode)
- `SN20.depthAt(clockMinutes)` -> 0..1, the accumulated snow depth for a clock time given in minutes after midnight (2:00 PM = 840; 11:00 PM = 1380; 7:00 AM next morning = 1860): 0 at 840, about .25 at 990 (4:30 PM), .45 at 1212 (8:12 PM), .6 at 1360, .9 at 1860 (a smooth curve, a little faster in the evening). `SN20.SCENE_CLOCK = { forecast: 840, lot: 990, blackout: 1092, dayroom: 1240, night: 1379 }` (the minute at the start of each scene; the night's time-lapse runs 1379 to 1860).
- `SN20.wind(t, gusts)` -> -1..1 sideways push, and `SN20.windInt(t, gusts)` its integral in px (what `snow` uses for drift). `gusts` = `[[t0, dur, amp], ...]` scripted gusts (a door opening, the plow); the base wind is a slow deterministic mix of two incommensurate sines. The sound builder does NOT need these numbers; it follows the script's cues (`doorgust`, `windgust`).

## Snow and cold
- `SN20.snow(ctx, o)` falling snow in a rectangle. `o = { x0, y0, x1, y1, t, seed, layers (1..4, default 3), density (1 = about 90 flakes per layer in a 1080 x 1920 rect), fall (speed multiplier; near flakes about 190 px/s, far about 60), wind (px/s sideways, default 0), gusts, sway (0..2, default 1), size (multiplier, flakes 2 to 14 px), alpha, tone, fore (default true: includes the large soft near layer), slice ('all' | 'back' | 'front': draw the far and mid layers behind your characters and the near layer in front of them) }`. Flakes wrap inside the rectangle, so a scene can fill a window pane, a doorway or the whole frame. Returns nothing.
- `SN20.frost(ctx, o)` frost on a window pane. `o = { x, y, w, h, amount (0..1), seed, t, tone }`: crystals (branching ferns) grow in from the pane's edges and the bottom corners as `amount` grows; 0 draws nothing; 1 covers about 60 percent of the pane with white lace and leaves the centre clear so the characters can see out; a faint glint travels across the crystals with `t`. Clip it to the pane yourself if the pane is not a rectangle.
- `SN20.sill(ctx, o)` a snow heap on a ledge. `o = { x0, x1, y, depth (px, the caller animates it), seed, tone }`: a lumpy profile along the ledge, soft blue shadow under the lip, a few sparkles. Draw it after the ledge.
- `SN20.bank(ctx, o)` a drift or mound. `o = { x, y (base centre), w, h, seed, shade (0..1, the blue shadow side), flip, tone }`.
- `SN20.cap(ctx, o)` snow lying along a flat top (a car roof, a sign, a bench, a hat brim): `o = { x0, x1, y, depth, seed, round (0..1), tone }`; draw it AFTER the object.
- `SN20.beam(ctx, o)` a headlight or flashlight beam in falling snow: `o = { x, y, ang, spread, len, color, alpha, t, seed, flakes (default true: the flakes inside the beam are brighter and sparkle), soft }`. Built on `FX18.cone`.
- `SN20.breath(ctx, o)` a puff of cold breath leaving a mouth: `o = { x, y, t, seed, period (default 1.9 s), dir (+1 right, -1 left), size, alpha, phase }`; thicker and softer than steam, with a faint darker edge so it reads on pale backgrounds.
- `SN20.spray(ctx, o)` snow thrown by a plow blade or a shovel: `o = { x, y, ang, t, t0, t1, seed, power, width, gravity }`; arcs and falls.
- `SN20.flurry(ctx, o)` a burst of powder (a snowball hit, a door dump, a slip): `o = { x, y, r, t, t0, life, seed, n }`.
- `SN20.settle(ctx, o)` snow settling into a shape (the title): `o = { pts (an array of [x, y] target points), from ({ x, y, r } the swirl's centre and radius), t, t0, t1, seed, size, alpha }`: flakes swirl around `from` until `t0`, then each flies to its target and rests, done at `t1`. `SN20.maskPoints(drawFn, w, h, step, seed)` returns target points sampled from whatever `drawFn(ctx)` paints (the title text, drawn once into an offscreen canvas), so the letters are made of flakes; the LAST frames hand over to the solid UI-kit text (the text gate reads the UI kit, not the flakes).

## Light: `L20.pass(ctx, o)`
A lighting pass over the picture already drawn: call it as the LAST world-space draw call of the scene's `draw` (camera transform active), or from `over()` with the same transform you used for the world; it converts every light's position through `ctx.getTransform()`, so give positions in the same units as the characters. It darkens everything except what the lights reach, tints the dark, adds the glow of the light sources and an optional grade.
- `o = { ambient (0..1: how much of the picture survives outside the lights; .22 = dim but readable, .0 = pitch black; never below .12 outside the pitch-black beat), tint ('#0b1230': the colour of the dark; default deep blue), lights: [...], bloom (0..1, glow around bright light cores, default .5), grade ({ color, alpha, mode } an optional final wash), vignette (0..1, default .25), lift (0..1: a floor under the darkness so faces never go black, default .08) }`.
- Light kinds, each `{ kind, x, y, color, power (0..1.5, default 1), flicker ({ seed, amt (0..1, keep it under .25), rate (Hz, keep it under 3) }) }`:
  - `'point'`: `r` (radius, px), `soft` (0..1, default .7): a lamp, a candle, a stopwatch glow, an LED.
  - `'cone'`: `ang` (rad), `spread` (rad), `len` (px), `soft`: a flashlight, a headlight, a ceiling downlight.
  - `'rect'`: `w`, `h`, `soft`: a window with moonlight, a TV, a laptop or clip screen (the light falls off in front of it; `facing` (rad) gives the direction it spills).
  - `'strip'`: `x2`, `y2`, `width`: a ceiling light strip, an exit sign's wash.
- Returns the number of lights drawn. A light with `power: 0` is skipped, so a scene can ramp lights on and off with `ss(lt, a, b)`.
- `L20.emergency(ctx, o)` SCREEN: the red-amber emergency wash for the generator beat: `o = { level (0..1), t, pulse (0..1, default .15: a slow breathing, never a strobe) }`.
- `L20.lamps(ctx, list, o)` a convenience: draws small glowing LED candles/lamps (the glow only, no darkening) for scenes that are lit but want a warm glow: `list = [{ x, y, r, color, flicker }]`.

## Also required of the kit
- `GUIDE_WX20.md` in the project folder: one paragraph and one example line per function, the options, the cost, the traps. Written FIRST (within 20 minutes, from this contract), refined at the end.
- A demo (`wx20_demo.html` plus `wx20_test.js`, not loaded by `index.html`) that renders every function at several times on a dark and on a pale background, and a determinism test (the same call twice is pixel-identical; frame t drawn first or last is identical).
- ORDER OF DELIVERY (each landed and `node --check` clean before the next; tell nobody, the files are read by everybody at once): 1 `L20.pass` (all four kinds) and `SN20.snow`, `depthAt`, `wind`; 2 `frost`, `sill`, `bank`, `cap`, `beam`, `breath`; 3 `spray`, `flurry`, `emergency`, `lamps`, `settle`, `maskPoints`.
