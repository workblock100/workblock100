# Brief E18-FX: the effects kit `ep18_fx.js` (window.FX18), then the title and end cards `ep18_title.js` (scenes `title`, `end`)

Read `CONTEXT18.md` and `FX18_API.md` first (the contract; names and shapes are fixed). You own: `ep18_fx.js`, `GUIDE_FX18.md`, `fx18_demo.html`, `fx18_test.js` (a test and demo harness, not loaded by `index.html`), `ep18_title.js` (scenes `title` and `end`), scratch in `build/ep18_fx/`.

## Part 1: the kit (first, and fast: four other builders are waiting on it)
1. Within 20 minutes: write `GUIDE_FX18.md` from the contract (one paragraph and one example call per function) and land the first file: the alarm pattern (`HORN`, `hornOn`, `strobe`) and the light passes (`wash`, `vignette`, `glow`, `cone`, `lamp`). Then in the order of FX18_API.md: `foamJet` and `foamPile`; `flame` and `embers`; `puff`, `sparks`, `smoke`; `water`, `smokeLayer`. After each batch run `node --check ep18_fx.js`, run your harness and look at a contact sheet of the batch (all effects at 3 times each on a neutral background AND over a character: draw a Strawberry from the Episode 15 to 17 scenes behind the effect to check that smoke or foam never reads as a flat blob over a face).
2. Quality bar for the effects (this is the first effects kit of the series; it has to look made, not scribbled): the flame has an inner white-yellow core, orange body, red edge, tongues that split and rejoin, a base glow on the surface it sits on and slow, uneven flicker; `kind:'log'` is a cosy Yule-log fire; the foam is thick and white with soft shading (a lighter top, a faint blue-grey shadow side), clumps that stick, bubbles at the edges, particles that grow and drift and a little settle; a jet has a bright dense core; smoke is soft and layered, never a hard-edged grey circle; sparks have tails; the strobe lamp has a hot white core, a bloom and a few thin rays, and its `level` 0 state is a dead lens; cones are soft-edged with a gentle falloff and slight noise so they do not look like triangles.
3. Determinism and cost: pure functions of `(args, t)` (no `Math.random`, no state), cached sprites, about 2 ms per effect per frame. The test: the same call twice gives identical pixels; frame t drawn after frame t+1 gives identical pixels; a 4-worker render and a 1-worker render of the same frames are identical (check with two preview PNGs of a demo scene, or in the harness).
4. When the kit has landed (all of FX18_API.md), post nothing extra: the other builders poll `GUIDE_FX18.md` and `typeof FX18.x`. Add anything a scene builder asks you for (they may message you through the lead; keep the signatures backwards compatible).

## Part 2: the cards (after the kit; the other builders' props `P18.exitSign`, `P18.pullStation`, `P18.hornStrobe`, `P18.smokeDetector`, `P18.drillLog` may have landed by then; if not, draw your own versions locally)
Study the model cards first: `ep17_title.js` (a learning-platform page stuck at 99 percent, end card as a module roster), `ep16_title.js`, `ep15_title.js` (title and end cards share a family: a hero title, a subtitle line, a cast or results sheet, a teaser line). Cue times: `python3 /tmp/shift_tools/cues.py 18 title` and `... end`.
- **title (6.8 s)**: a painted hospital-green corridor wall in a close, slightly dim frame. Above, a green EXIT sign in the series' style lettered SHIFT HAPPENS (the glowing exit-sign look: the running-figure icon is a running strawberry; the sign flickers on at 0.3 s). Below it, a red pull station (white lettering FIRE, the lever PULL DOWN; a small laminated card taped beside it: EPISODE 18 / THE FIRE DRILL, UI kit label). A small hand (a strawberry glove) reaches in and pulls the lever at `pullclick` (2.2 s + beat start offset; snap to the frame): the lever drops with a tiny overshoot, the horn starts at `horn3` 2.3 s; from then the wall strobes and the sign's glow pulse with the horn pattern (`FX18.hornOn`, `FX18.strobe`, `FX18.lamp`, `FX18.wash` at low alpha, 3 pulses at 2.3, 3.3, 4.3 s), the title letters drop 3 px on each pulse; the horn stops (`hornstop` 5.25 s), the sign stays lit and a `textding` at 5.4 s lights the subtitle card. Nothing static: dust in the strobe light, the sign buzzes (tiny flicker), the camera drifts in 2 percent.
- **end (7.5 s)**: the drill log as the end card, a clipboard sheet on the wall under a smoke detector. Rows (UI kit table): R RESCUE: MED CART (check), A ALARM: SNOOZED, C CONTAIN: PENDING, E EVACUATE: 80 OF 40, TIME: 0 MIN, SIGNED: MONDAY; a PASS stamp lands at `stamp` (3.9 s). The cast roll as small chips if the family does it (as in `ep17_title.js`). The smoke detector in the top corner chirps (its LED blinks) at the `chirp` cues (2.1, 4.6 and 7.0 s) with a small `FX18.glow`; a teaser line: NEXT: EPISODE 19 / THE POTLUCK (constant `TEASER19 = 'THE POTLUCK'` at the top of the file, and `window.FP18A` exports like `FP15A` if the family does it). Hold the final frame calm: the chirp light is the only motion at the end besides a slow drift.
Gate both with `bash /tmp/shift_tools/audit_scene.sh 18 title` and `... end` (`0 issue-frames`).
