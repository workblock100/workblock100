Warm interior light against the cool blue afternoon sky. Rounded, friendly shapes, like the characters. Soft contact shadows, a subtle vignette per room, a few dust motes in the light. Everything drawn at 2x into cached canvases per room where it is static (no text in caches); animate the live parts (lamps, a TV flicker, a ticking clock, a dripping tap, call-light domes) on top.
